# Tomato Soy

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount   | Notes |
| --------------- | -------- | ----- |
| Green Tomatoes  | 1 Peck   | Sliced |
| Onions          | 2 Quarts | Thin |
| Salt            | 1 Cup    | For overnight |
| Cider Vinegar   | 3 Cups   | For syrup |
| Brown Sugar     | 4 lbs    | For syrup |
| Dry Mustard     | 2 Tbsp   | |
| Celery Seed     | 2 Tbsp   | |
| Whole Cloves    | 2 Tbsp   | |
| Stick Cinnamon  | 2        | Broken up |
| Powdered Ginger | 2 tsp    | |
| Salt            | 1 tsp    | |
| Garlic Powder   | 1 tsp    | |
| Green Peppers   | 12       | Sweet |
| Red Peppers     | 6        | Sweet |

## Method

1. Slice green tomatoes and thin onions
1. Sprinkle with 1 cup salt and set at least 12 hours
1. In the morning, wash with clean water to remove salt, drain in colanders
1. Make a syrup: bring cider vinegar and brown sugar to a boil in a large preserving kettle
1. Put spices (dry mustard, celery seed, whole cloves, stick cinnamon, powdered ginger, salt) in a saucer, add garlic powder
1. Add drained tomatoes and onions to the syrup
1. Cut peppers in strips, circles, or dice and add
1. Bring to a boil and simmer 1 hour
1. Put in hot sterilized jars — pack with tomatoes, then fill up with syrup
1. Makes 9 quarts

## Notes From Gran

* 1 peck = ¼ bushel
* Use non-iodized salt

## Modern Notes

* Tomato soy is an old-fashioned spiced green tomato relish — "soy" here refers to a soy-sauce-like condiment, not soybeans
* A peck of tomatoes is about 12–14 lbs
* The sweet-sour-spicy flavor makes this a wonderful condiment for meats
* Use proper canning procedures for safe preservation
