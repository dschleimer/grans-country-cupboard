# Pineapple Coconut Squares

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Crushed Pineapple   | 1 Can  | #2 can |
| Angel Flake Coconut | 1 Can  | 3½ oz |
| Brown Sugar         | 1 Cup  | |
| Yellow Cake Mix     | ½ pkg  | |
| Margarine           | ¼ lb   | |
| Chopped Nuts        | 1 Cup  | |

## Method

1. Drain crushed pineapple, reserving the juice
1. In a 13×9-inch pan, sprinkle coconut and spread pineapple over it
1. Sprinkle brown sugar over the pineapple
1. Add half a box of dry yellow cake mix and spread evenly
1. Cut margarine into thin slices and dot over the cake mix
1. Sprinkle chopped nuts on top
1. Bake at 300°F for 1 hour
1. Cut into squares and serve warm with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream
