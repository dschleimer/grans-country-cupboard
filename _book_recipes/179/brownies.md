# Brownies

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount    | Notes |
| -------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Chocolate      | 2 Squares | Melted in double boiler |
| Butter         | ¼ lb      | |
| Sugar          | 1 Cup     | |
| Eggs           | 2         | |
| Flour          | ½ Cup     | |
| Baking Powder  | ½ tsp     | |
| Salt           | ¼ tsp     | |
| Chopped Pecans | ½ Cup     | |
| Vanilla        | 1 tsp     | |

## Method

1. Melt chocolate in a double boiler with butter
1. Add sugar and beat in eggs
1. Add sifted flour, baking powder, and salt
1. Add chopped pecans and vanilla
1. Pour into a buttered and floured square pan
1. Bake at 350°F until done — test with the pressure of a finger
1. Do not overbake
1. When done, remove from oven and cut in pan with a knife dipped in cold water first
1. Put brownies back in oven for 1 minute
1. With a spatula, remove from pan to a cake rack
1. Serve fresh — these freeze well
