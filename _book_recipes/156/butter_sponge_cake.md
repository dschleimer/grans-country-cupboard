# Butter Sponge Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Egg Yolks     | 11      | |
| Sifted Sugar  | 2 Cups  | |
| Scalded Milk  | 1 Cup   | |
| Vanilla       | 1 tsp   | |
| Sifted Flour  | 2½ Cups | |
| Baking Powder | 2 tsp   | |
| Melted Butter | ⅓ Cup   | |

## Method

1. Beat egg yolks and sugar until light and very fluffy
1. Add scalded milk and vanilla, beating constantly
1. Sift flour, baking powder together and fold into the mixture
1. Add the creamy mixture and fold in melted butter
1. Batter will be very thin
1. Pour into 2 well-greased 9-inch layer cake pans
1. Bake at 350°F for 30–40 minutes
1. Invert pans and cool before removing

## Notes From Gran

* Makes a Bundt cake — dust with confectioners sugar
* Fill pan no more than ⅔ full, use extra for cupcakes

## Modern Notes

* This is an egg-yolk-rich sponge cake — a great way to use up yolks if you have leftover whites from another recipe
* The large number of egg yolks gives a very rich, golden cake
