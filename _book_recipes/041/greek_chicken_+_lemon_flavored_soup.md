# Greek Chicken + Lemon Flavored Soup

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount       | Notes |
| ---------- | ------------ | ----- |
| Chicken    | 1 baking hen | Cut in half |
| Onion      | 1 large      | Quartered |
| Celery     | 1 stalk      | Complete including leaves |
| Salt       |              | |
| Pepper     |              | |
| Eggs       | 4            | Separated |
| Lemons     | 2            | Juiced |
| Rice       | 3 cups       | |

## Method

1. Cut baking hen in half
1. Cover with water
1. Quarter onion and add to pot
1. Add celery to pot
1. Add salt and pepper to taste
1. Simmer for 4 hours
1. Drain, reserving broth and optionally chicken
1. Discard onion and celery
1. Beat egg whites to stiff peaks
1. Beat in one yolk at a time
1. Juice lemons and slowly add juice to soup
1. In a separate pot, boil 2 cups of raw rice
1. Drain rice
1. Transfer soup to blender and add drained rice
1. Blend

## Notes
* Warm to taste (about 15 minutes) before serving
* Slice chicken for sandwiches if desired

## Modern Notes
* The original recipe did not explictly say to blend, but heavily implied it by saying "add \[rice\] to other ingredients in blender"  -- David S
