# Jacobi Grape Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount  | Notes |
| -------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Concord Grapes | 7 Cups  | |
| Cornstarch     | 3 Tbsp  | |
| Sugar          | 1½ Cups | |
| Salt           | ¾ tsp   | |
| Orange         | 1       | Grated rind |
| Pastry         |         | For lattice top |

## Method

1. Wash and stem the grapes
1. Slip the skins off the grapes and set the skins aside
1. Heat the grape pulp to boiling and boil for 5 minutes
1. Put through a sieve to remove the seeds
1. Combine cornstarch, sugar, salt, and grated orange rind with the grape pulp
1. Cook, stirring constantly, until thickened
1. Add the reserved grape skins and stir in
1. Pour into a pastry-lined pie pan
1. Top with ⅜-inch strips of pastry in a lattice pattern
1. Bake at 425°F for 10 minutes, then reduce to 325°F for 20 minutes more

## Modern Notes
