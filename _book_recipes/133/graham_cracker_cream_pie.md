# Graham Cracker Cream Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount | Notes |
| --------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Milk                  | 2 Cups | For filling |
| Egg Yolks             | 2      | For filling |
| Flour                 | 3 Tbsp | For filling |
| Sugar                 | ½ Cup  | For filling |
| Vanilla               | 1 tsp  | For filling |
| Large Graham Crackers | 17     | For crust |
| Flour                 | 1 Tbsp | For crust |
| Sugar                 | ¼ Cup  | For crust |
| Melted Butter         | ⅓ Cup  | For crust |
| Vanilla               | 1 tsp  | For crust |
| Cinnamon              |        | Dash, for crust |
| Egg Whites            | 3      | For meringue |
| Confectioners Sugar   | 6 Tbsp | For meringue |
| Vanilla               | 1 tsp  | For meringue |

## Method

1. Cook milk, egg yolks, 3 Tbsp flour, and ½ cup sugar together, stirring until the mixture coats a silver spoon
1. Add 1 tsp vanilla and set aside to cool
1. Crush graham crackers and mix with 1 Tbsp flour, ¼ cup sugar, melted butter, 1 tsp vanilla, and a dash of cinnamon
1. Pat the crumb mixture into a pie pan
1. Fill with the cooled custard filling
1. Beat egg whites until stiff, then beat in confectioners sugar and 1 tsp vanilla to make meringue
1. Cover the pie with meringue
1. Brown in the oven at 300°F for 15–25 minutes
1. Chill before serving

## Modern Notes

* "Coats a silver spoon" means the custard is thick enough that when you dip a spoon and run your finger across the back, it leaves a clean line — about 170°F
