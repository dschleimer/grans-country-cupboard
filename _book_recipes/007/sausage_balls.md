# Sausage Balls

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount | Notes |
| -------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Prepared Biscuit Mix | 3 cups | |
| Hot or Mild Sausage  | 1 lb   | |
| Sharp Cheese         | ½ lb   | |

## Prepare in Advance
1. Mix biscuit as directed on package
1. Add Sausage & cheese
1. Make into small balls
1. Freeze on cookie sheet then put into plastic bag
1. Remove amount wanted when needed

## Before Serving
1. Preheat oven to 350°F
1. Thaw 10 min
1. Bake 20-25 minutes

## Notes From Gran
* Serve Hot
