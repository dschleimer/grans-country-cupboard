# Old Fashioned Strawberry Dumplings

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Sugar         | ½ Cup   | For berry mixture |
| Strawberries  | 2 Pints | Hulled |
| Sugar         | 5 Tbsp  | For topping |
| Vanilla       | ½ tsp   | Set aside |
| Flour         | 1 Cup   | |
| Salt          | ½ tsp   | |
| Baking Powder | ½ tsp   | |
| Butter        | 2 Tbsp  | |
| Milk          | ½ Cup   | |

## Method

1. Combine ½ cup sugar and strawberries in a baking dish, then add 5 Tbsp sugar and ½ tsp vanilla — set aside
1. Sift flour, salt, and baking powder together
1. Cut in butter with a fork, stir in milk until just combined
1. Arrange strawberries in a buttered casserole dish
1. Pour hot sugar mixture over the berries
1. Drop dumpling dough by spoonfuls over the berries — about 8–10 spoonfuls
1. Sprinkle with a little sugar
1. Bake at 450°F for 25–30 minutes or until done
1. Serve warm with cream

## Notes From Gran

* Squash the berries slightly before putting in casserole

## Modern Notes
