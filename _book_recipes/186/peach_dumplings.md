# Peach Dumplings

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Flour         | 1 Quart | |
| Salt          | ¼ tsp   | |
| Baking Powder | 4 tsp   | |
| Sugar         | ½ Cup   | |
| Butter        | 2 Tbsp  | |
| Milk          |         | Enough for soft dough |
| Peaches       |         | Ripe, peeled and cut up |
| Cinnamon      |         | For sprinkling |
| Sugar         |         | For sprinkling |
| Water         | 3 Cups  | For sauce |
| Brown Sugar   | ½ Cup   | For sauce |
| Butter        |         | For sauce, boiled |

## Method

1. Mix flour, salt, baking powder, and sugar together
1. Cut in butter
1. Add milk to make a soft dough
1. Roll out on a floured board to about ½ inch thick
1. Spread with soft butter
1. Place peeled and cut-up ripe peaches on the dough
1. Sprinkle with sugar and a little cinnamon
1. Roll up and cut like cinnamon buns
1. Put into a fairly deep pan in which you have boiled 3 cups water, ½ cup brown sugar, and butter
1. Bake at 425°F for ½ hour or until done
1. Bake in sauce — serve cold with plain or whipped cream

## Modern Notes

* These are baked fruit dumplings — like a fruit-filled cinnamon roll baked in a sweet sauce
* The sauce bubbles up around the dumplings during baking for a caramelized finish
* Can also be made with apples or other stone fruit in season
