# Ham with Vegetables

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount                      | Notes |
| ---------- | --------------------------- | ----- |
| Butter     | ¼ cup                       | |
| Mushrooms  | 12 oz fresh or 8oz can      | |
| Flour      | 1 Tbsp                      | |
| Sherry     | 1 Tbsp                      | |
| Sour Cream | 1 cup                       | |
| Onions     | 2 Tbsp                      | Chopped - Use onions or Chives |
| Chives     | 2 Tbsp                      | Chopped - Use onions or Chives |
| Ham        | 2 cups                      | Cooked and cubed |
| Peas       | 2 cups peas + carrots total | |
| Carrots    | 2 cups peas + carrots total | |

## Method

1. Melt butter
1. Saute onions
1. Add mushrooms and cook 5 minutes
1. Add flour and cook 2 minutes
1. Stir in ham and vegetables
1. Season to taste
1. Add sherry and sour cream
1. Heat gently
1. Serve
