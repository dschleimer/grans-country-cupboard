# Rice Crispie Crunch

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butterscotch Bits | 1 pkg  | Small |
| Peanut Butter     | ½ Cup  | |
| Rice Crispies     | 3 Cups | |

## Method

1. Melt butterscotch bits and peanut butter together in a double boiler
1. Stir in 3 cups Rice Crispies
1. Press into a buttered 9×9-inch pan
1. No baking required

*

## Modern Notes

* This is a quick no-bake treat — similar to Rice Krispie treats but with butterscotch and peanut butter instead of marshmallow
* Can also be dropped by spoonfuls onto wax paper instead of pressed into a pan
