# Helen Corder's Fudge

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount    | Notes |
| ------------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Confectioners Sugar | 1 Box     | 1 lb |
| Evaporated Milk     | 1 Can     | Small |
| Chocolate           | 2 Squares | |
| Butter              | ⅛ lb      | |
| Marshmallow Whip    | 4 oz      | |
| Vanilla             | 1 tsp     | |

## Method

1. Cook confectioners sugar, evaporated milk, chocolate, and butter together for 8 minutes after it starts to boil, stirring constantly
1. Remove from heat
1. Add marshmallow whip and vanilla
1. Beat until smooth
1. Pour into a buttered pan
1. Cool
