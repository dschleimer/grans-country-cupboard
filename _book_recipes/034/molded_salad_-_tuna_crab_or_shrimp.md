# Molded Salad - Tuna, Crab or Shrimp

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount      | Notes |
| ------------ | ----------- | ----- |
| Lemon Jello  | 3 oz        | Recipe wrote it as 1-3 oz, presumably referring to one packet of the 3oz size |
| Hot Water    | 1 cup       | |
| Chunk tuna   | 14 oz       | Recipe calls for two 7 oz cans |
| Pimento      | 1 small jar | Diced |
| Cream Cheese | 8 oz        | |
| Onion        | 1 Tbsp      | Minced |
| Green Pepper | 2 Tbsp      | Diced |
| Celery       | ½ cup       | Diced |
| Half + Half  | ½ cup       | |
| Mayonnaise   | ¼ cup       | |
| Eggs         | 3           | Hard boiled - Cut up |
| Salt         | ¼ tsp       | |

## Method

1. Hard boil eggs, allow to cool
1. Cut up eggs
1. Mince onion
1. Dice Green Pepper and Celery
1. Add hot water to Jello and allow to jell slightly
1. Mix together Mayonnaise, Cream Cheese, and Milk
1. Add all ingredients except for jello to the mixture
1. Add mixture to jello
1. Pour into mold
1. Chill several hours or overnight
