# Turkey Lasagne

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Mushroom Soup | 1 can | |
| Milk | 2/3 cup | |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp | |
| Poultry Seasoning | 1/2 tsp | |
| Cream Cheese | 6 oz | Cream cheese used to be sold in 3oz "small" packages.  The recipe called for 2 |
| Cottage Cheese | 1 cup | |
| Stuffed Olives | 1/2 cup | Sliced |
| Onion | 1/3 cup | Chopped |
| Green Pepper | 1/3 cup | Cut up |
| Parsley | 1/4 cup | |
| Turkey | 3 cup | Cubed |
| Noodles | 3 cup | Cooked |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 375F
1. Cook noodles
1. Slice olives
1. Chop Onion
1. Cut up green pepper
1. Cut up parsley
1. Cube turkey if not pre-cubed
1. Mix together mushroom soup, milk, salt and poultry seasoning
1. Add Cream cheese olives, onion, green pepper, parsley, and turkey
1. Place half the noodles into casserole dish
1. Then the half the mixture
1. Then the other half of the noodles and the other half of the mixture
1. Bake 30 minutes
