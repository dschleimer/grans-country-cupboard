# Eye Roast Beef

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount | Notes |
| -------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Rib Eye Roas   |        | |
| Onion Soup Mix |        | Lipton's Brand |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 375 °F
1. Sprinkle onion soup mix on roast
1. Wrap tightly in foil
1. Put in roasting pan
1. Roast 1 hour per lb

## Notes From Gran

* Make Gravy with Juices
* Slice thin
