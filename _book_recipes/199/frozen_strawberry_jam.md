# Frozen Strawberry Jam

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Water            | ¾ Cup   | |
| Sure-Jell        | 1 Box   | |
| Mashed Berries   | 4 Cups  | |
| White Karo Syrup | 1 Cup   | |
| Sugar            | 5½ Cups | |

## Method

1. Mix water and Sure-Jell, bring to a boil for 1 minute
1. Add 4 cups mashed berries — mash each batch separately with a fork, catching the juice
1. Set at room temperature for 20 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes
1. Add 1 cup white Karo syrup and 5½ cups sugar
1. Let stand until sugar dissolves
1. Freeze — makes 5 pints
