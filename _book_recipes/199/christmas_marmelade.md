# Christmas Marmelade

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Oranges    | 10     | Large |
| Lemons     | 3      | |
| Grapefruit | 2      | Preferably pink |
| Tangerines | 2      | |
| Sugar      |        | Equal amount to fruit |

## Method

1. Wash and remove any skin discolorations with a knife
1. Remove seeds and hard center pulp
1. Put all the fruit through a meat grinder — save all the juice
1. Add an equal amount of water
1. Let stand overnight
1. Next day, boil 2 hours slowly until just tender
1. Add an equal amount of sugar — about 10 cups
1. Boil 1 hour in a large kettle or until it jells
1. Either put in sterilized jars or seal with hot paraffin
