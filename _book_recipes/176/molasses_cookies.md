# Molasses Cookies

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sifted Flour            | 3 Cups | |
| Baking Powder           | 2 tsp  | |
| Baking Soda             | ½ tsp  | |
| Salt                    | ½ tsp  | |
| Cinnamon                | ½ tsp  | |
| Nutmeg                  | ½ tsp  | |
| Cloves                  | ½ tsp  | |
| Butter                  | ½ Cup  | |
| Brown Sugar             | ¼ Cup  | |
| Molasses                | ¾ Cup  | |
| Sour Milk or Buttermilk | ¼ Cup  | |
| Vanilla                 | 1 tsp  | |

## Method

1. Sift flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves together
1. Cream butter and brown sugar
1. Add molasses and mix well
1. Add dry ingredients alternately with sour milk or buttermilk
1. Add vanilla
1. Mix, then chill the dough
1. Place on a lightly floured board and roll ⅛ inch thick
1. Cut with a cookie cutter
1. Bake at 375°F for about 12 minutes
1. Makes about 100 cookies

## Modern Notes

* These are thin, crispy rolled molasses cookies — the rolling and cutting makes them more elegant than drop cookies
* To make sour milk, add 1 tsp vinegar or lemon juice to ¼ cup milk and let stand 5 minutes
* Chill the dough well before rolling — at least 1 hour
