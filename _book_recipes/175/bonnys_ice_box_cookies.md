# Bonny's Ice Box Cookies

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butter           | ½ lb   | |
| Dark Brown Sugar | 1 lb   | |
| Eggs             | 2      | |
| Flour            | 2 Cups | More if necessary |
| Baking Soda      | 1 tsp  | |
| Salt             | ¼ tsp  | |
| Chopped Nuts     | 1 Cup  | |

## Method

1. Cream butter and dark brown sugar together
1. Add eggs and mix well
1. Sift together flour, baking soda, and salt
1. Add dry ingredients and mix — add more flour if necessary
1. Stir in chopped nuts
1. Form dough into a roll and wrap in wax paper
1. Set in refrigerator overnight
1. In the morning, slice very thin
1. Bake at 425°F for 3–5 minutes
