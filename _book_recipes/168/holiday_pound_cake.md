# Holiday Pound Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient             | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Butter                 | 2 Cups  | |
| Sugar                  | 2¼ Cups | |
| Vanilla                | 2 tsp   | |
| Lemon Peel             | 1 tsp   | Grated |
| Eggs                   | 9       | Large, add one at a time |
| Candied Red Cherries   | 1 Cup   | Chopped |
| Candied Green Cherries | 1 Cup   | Chopped |
| Pecans                 | 1 Cup   | Finely chopped |
| Flour                  | 3½ Cups | Plus 1 cup for tossing |
| Baking Powder          | 1½ tsp  | |

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar well
1. Blend in vanilla and grated lemon peel
1. Add eggs one at a time, beating after each addition
1. Toss the chopped cherries, green cherries, and pecans with 1 cup flour to coat
1. Sift remaining 3½ cups flour with baking powder
1. Add the sifted flour mixture gradually, about ½ cup at a time
1. Fold in the flour-coated cherries, nuts, and flour
1. Pour into a greased and floured 10-inch tube pan
1. Bake at 325°F for 1 hour and 45 minutes or until done
1. Rest on a rack for 10 minutes, then remove from pan to cool completely

## Notes From Gran

* Will keep several weeks stored in a tight container in the refrigerator
