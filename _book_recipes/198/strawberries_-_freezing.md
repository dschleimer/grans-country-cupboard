# Strawberries - Freezing

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount | Notes |
| ------------ | ------ | ----- |
| Strawberries |        | Fresh, with caps |
| Sugar        |        | A little |

## Method

1. Pick local berries with caps attached
1. Refrigerate as soon as possible
1. When ready to use, wash the berries gently — do not soak
1. Remove caps
1. Use berries whole, sliced, or crushed, adding a little sugar
