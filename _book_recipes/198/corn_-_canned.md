# Corn - Canned

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Raw Corn   | 7 Cups | Cut off cob |
| Salt       | ½ tsp  | |
| Sugar      | 1 tsp  | |

## Method

1. Cut corn off the cob — 7 cups
1. Add salt and sugar
1. Set and let stand to draw juices
1. Boil 30 minutes, then put in sterilized jars
1. Fill jars completely full with liquid and water
1. Seal

## Modern Notes

* Home canning corn requires a pressure canner — corn is a low-acid food and must be pressure processed, not water-bath canned -- Claude (AI)
* Modern USDA guidelines recommend processing pint jars at 10 lbs pressure for 55 minutes -- Claude (AI)
