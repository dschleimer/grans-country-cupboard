# Apple Sauce - Canned

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount   | Notes |
| ---------- | -------- | ----- |
| Apples     | 4 Quarts | |
| Sugar      |          | To taste |

## Method

1. Pare and core apples
1. Cook with a very little water until done
1. Take from stove and beat until smooth
1. Put back on stove and boil while bottling
1. Use only sterilized jars
1. Sugar may be added when jar is opened

## Modern Notes

* This is a simple home-canned applesauce — no sugar added during canning
* Use proper canning procedures — sterilize jars and process in a boiling water bath for safe preservation
* Adding sugar at serving time lets each person sweeten to taste
