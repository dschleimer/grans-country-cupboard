# Preserved Strawberries

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Strawberries     | 1 Quart | Washed, stemmed |
| Granulated Sugar | 2 lbs   | |

## Method

1. Wash and stem berries
1. Layer strawberries and granulated sugar in a pot
1. Set and stand overnight, stirring occasionally
1. In the morning, bring slowly to a boil
1. Boil 10 minutes — the berries should boil briskly
1. Cool before preserving
1. Do only 1 quart at a time

## Modern Notes

* This is an old-fashioned strawberry preserve — the overnight sugar maceration draws out juices and keeps berries whole
* Doing only 1 quart at a time ensures even cooking and proper set
* Store in sterilized jars using proper canning procedures
