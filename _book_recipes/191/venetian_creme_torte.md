# Venetian Crème Torte

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Flour               | 2 Cups  | For cake |
| Cocoa               | ½ Cup   | For cake |
| Baking Soda         | ½ tsp   | For cake |
| Salt                | ⅛ tsp   | For cake |
| Butter              | ½ Cup   | For cake |
| Vanilla             | 1 tsp   | For cake |
| Sugar               | 1⅓ Cups | For cake |
| Eggs                | 3       | Well beaten |
| Buttermilk          | 1 Cup   | For cake |
| Sugar               | ½ Cup   | For filling |
| Cornstarch          | ¼ Cup   | For filling |
| Salt                | ½ tsp   | For filling |
| Milk                | 1⅓ Cups | For filling |
| Heavy Cream         | 1 Cup   | For filling |
| Butter              | ½ Cup   | For filling |
| Vanilla             | 1 tsp   | For filling |
| Confectioners Sugar | ½ Cup   | For filling |

## Method

1. Sift flour, cocoa, baking soda, and salt together
1. Cream butter, vanilla, and sugar until very fluffy, then add slowly
1. Add well-beaten eggs to the creamed mixture
1. Alternately add dry ingredients and buttermilk, beat until smooth
1. Turn batter into 3 greased 9-inch round cake pans lined with greased wax paper
1. Bake at 350°F for 18 minutes or until done
1. Cool 10 minutes, remove from pans, cool completely
1. Split each cake layer to form 6 layers
1. For the filling: combine sugar, cornstarch, and salt, then gradually add milk, stirring constantly over heat
1. Stir in heavy cream and butter
1. Add vanilla and confectioners sugar, beat until fluffy
1. Chill the filling, then set over ice water and whip until fluffy
1. Spread filling between all layers and on top
1. Chill before serving
