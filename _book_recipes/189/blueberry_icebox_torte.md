# Blueberry Icebox Torte

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Lady Fingers        | 2½ doz | Split |
| Blueberries         | 2 Cans | 12 oz each |
| Tapioca             | ½ Cup  | |
| Confectioners Sugar | ¾ Cup  | |
| Lemons              | 2      | Juiced |
| Heavy Cream         | 1 qt   | Whipped |
| Chopped Pecans      | 1 Cup  | |
| Macaroons           | 1 doz  | Crumbled |

## Method

1. Butter the bottom and sides of a 9-inch springform pan
1. Line the pan with split lady fingers — around the sides and on the bottom
1. Drain blueberries, reserving the juice
1. Cook the juice with sugar, tapioca, lemon juice, and a little cornstarch, stirring constantly until thickened
1. Cool, then add drained blueberries
1. Spread ⅓ of the blueberry mixture over the lady fingers
1. Alternate layers of lady fingers, blueberry mixture, whipped cream, and crumbled macaroons
1. Top layer should be whipped cream, sprinkled with chopped nuts
1. Refrigerate 12 hours before serving

## Notes From Gran

* Use fresh or frozen blueberries

## Modern Notes

* This is an elegant no-bake icebox torte — the lady fingers soften overnight from the fruit juices -- Claude (AI)
* A springform pan is essential for unmolding -- Claude (AI)
* Must chill at least 12 hours for the layers to set properly -- Claude (AI)
