# New Jersey Blueberry Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Butter        | ½ Cup   | |
| Sugar         | 1 Cup   | |
| Egg Yolks     | 2       | Beaten |
| Flour         | 1½ Cups | |
| Salt          | ½ tsp   | |
| Baking Powder | 1 tsp   | |
| Milk          | ⅓ Cup   | |
| Vanilla       | 1 tsp   | |
| Egg Whites    | 2       | Beaten stiff |
| Blueberries   | 1 Cup   | Washed and well drained |
| Blueberries   | ½ Cup   | For sauce |
| Sugar         | ½ Cup   | For sauce |
| Cornstarch    | 1 Tbsp  | For sauce |
| Boiling Water | 1 Cup   | For sauce |
| Lemon Juice   | ½ tsp   | For sauce |
| Butter        | 1 Tbsp  | For sauce |

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar, then add beaten egg yolks
1. Add flour, salt, and baking powder alternately with milk
1. Add vanilla
1. Fold in stiffly beaten egg whites
1. Line a cake pan with wax paper, butter the pan, and spread the batter evenly
1. Sprinkle with 1 cup washed, well-drained blueberries
1. Add remaining batter on top
1. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour
1. For the sauce: mix ½ cup sugar and 1 Tbsp cornstarch, add 1 cup boiling water, stir constantly
1. Add ½ cup blueberries and cook until clear and thickened
1. Add ½ tsp lemon juice and 1 Tbsp butter
1. Serve warm over the cake
