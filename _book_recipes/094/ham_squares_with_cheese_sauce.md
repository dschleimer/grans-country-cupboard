# Ham Squares with Cheese Sauce

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount   | Notes |
| ----------------------- | -------- | ----- |
| Ground Ham              | 2 lbs    | Smoked |
| Eggs                    | 4        | Beaten |
| Milk                    | 2 cups   | |
| Onion                   | 1 small  | minced |
| Prepared Mustard        | 2 Tbsp   | |
| Processed Cheese Spread | 8 oz jar | |
| White Wine              | ⅓ cup    | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F
1. Grease 8 inch square pan
1. Mince onion
1. Beat eggs
1. Mix together ham, eggs, milk, onion, and mustard
1. Pour into greased pan
1. Bake 40-45 minutes or until firm to the touch in the center
1. In a saucepan heat cheese spread and white wine
1. Cut ham mixture into squares
1. Cover squares with sauce

## Notes From Gran

* Serve hot
* Good with Asparagus and Pumpernickel bread
