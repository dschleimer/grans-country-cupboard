# Lobster Thermidor

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount                  | Notes |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- | ----- |
| Lobster              | 2 Tails                 | 1½ to 2 lbs each |
| Butter               | ¼ cup + 2 Tbsp + 2 Tbsp | Butter goes in three separate times |
| Flour                | 2 Tbsp                  | |
| Prepared Mustard     | 1 tsp                   | |
| Salt                 | ½ tsp                   | |
| Paprika              | 1 tsp                   | |
| Cayenne              | dash                    | |
| Cream                | 1½ cup                  | Use cream or Half+ Half, not both |
| Half + Half          | 1½ cup                  | Use cream or Half+ Half, not both |
| Cheddar Cheese       | 2 cups                  | Shredded |
| Worcestershire Sauce | 1 tsp                   | |
| Green Peppers        | ¼ cup                   | Diced |
| Mushrooms            | ½ lb                    | Sliced - Fresh or Canned |
| Cracker              | 2 Tbsp                  | Use Cracker or bread crumbs, not both |
| Bread Crumbs         | 2 Tbsp                  | Use Cracker or bread crumbs, not both |
| Parmesan             | 2 Tbsp                  | |

## Method

#### Cook Lobster
1. Salt water and bring to a rapid boil
1. Cook tails 10-12 minutes
1. Remove meat from shells, preserving shells
1. Cut meat into bite-sized pieces

#### Prepare Cheese Sauce
1. Shred Cheddar Cheese if not pre-shredded
1. In a saucepan, melt 2 Tbsp of butter
1. Blend in Flour, Prepared Mustard, Salt, Paprika, and Cayenne
1. Gradually add cream or Half + Half
1. Cook, stirring constantly until thickened
1. Turn off heat
1. Add Shredded Cheddar Cheese and Worcestershire Sauce
1. Stir until Cheese Melts

#### Prepare Mushrooms and Peppers
1. Slice Mushrooms if not pre-sliced
1. Dice Green Peppers
1. In a small fry pan, melt ¼ cup butter
1. Sauté Green Peppers and Mushrooms about 5 minutes

#### Fill the Shells
1. Combine Lobster, Cheese Sauce, and Sauteed Mushrooms/Peppers
1. Fill shells
1. Melt 2 Tbsp butter
1. Top each shell with:
  * 1 Tbsp of Cracker or Bread Crumbs
  * 1 Tbsp of Melted butter
  * 2 Tbsp of Parmesan
1. Broil filled shells until sauce is bubbly and lightly browned
