# Asparagus + Lemon Casserole

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount  | Notes |
| --------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Chicken Breasts       | 4       | Boneless - Skinless - Cooked - Cut bite size |
| Cream of Chicken Soup | 2 cans  | |
| Lemon Juice           | 2 tsp   | |
| Mayonnaise            | 3/4 cup | Recipe specifies Hellman's Blue Ribbon Mayonnaise |
| Asparagus             | 2 boxes | Cut - Cooked |
| Bread Crumps          |         | |
| Butter                |         | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350F
1. Debone, deskin, cook, and cut chicken into bite-sized pieces, if needed
1. Cook, drain, and cut up asparagus
1. Grease Casserole dish
1. Add chicken and asparagus to casserole
1. Mix other ingredients and spoon over chicken mixture
1. Sprinkle breadcrumbs over mixture
1. Dot with butter
1. Bake until bubbly - about 20 minutes

## Notes From Gran

* THis may be prepared ahead of time
