# Fresh Fruit Cobbler

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Fresh Fruit   | 3 Cups | Peaches, raspberries, or strawberries |
| Sugar         | ½ Cup  | For fruit |
| Flour         | 2 Tbsp | For fruit - Use Flour or Quick Tapioca, not both |
| Quick Tapioca | 2 Tbsp | For fruit - Use Flour or Quick Tapioca, not both |
| Cinnamon      | ½ tsp  | For fruit |
| Butter        | 2 Tbsp | For dotting fruit |
| Flour         | 2 Cups | For topping |
| Sugar         | 2 Tbsp | For topping |
| Baking Powder | ½ tsp  | For topping |
| Salt          | ½ tsp  | For topping |
| Shortening    | ½ Cup  | For topping |
| Milk          | 3 Tbsp | For topping |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
1. Place 3 cups sliced fresh fruit in a 9×9-inch pan
1. Sprinkle fruit with mixture of ½ cup sugar, 2 Tbsp flour or quick tapioca, and ½ tsp cinnamon
1. Dot with 2 Tbsp butter
1. Sift together 2 cups flour, 2 Tbsp sugar, baking powder, and salt
1. Add shortening and milk, stir just to blend
1. Drop by spoonfuls over the fruit or cover evenly
1. Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes
1. Serve warm with cream

## Notes From Gran

* May be doubled — in a larger pan bake at 350°F for 40–50 minutes
