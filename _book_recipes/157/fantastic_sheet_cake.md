# Fantastic Sheet Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Flour               | 2 Cups | For cake |
| Sugar               | 2 Cups | For cake |
| Cocoa               | 3 Tbsp | For cake |
| Butter              | ¼ lb   | For cake |
| Shortening          | ¼ Cup  | For cake |
| Water               | 1 Cup  | For cake |
| Eggs                | 2      | For cake |
| Buttermilk          | ½ Cup  | For cake |
| Vanilla             | 1 tsp  | For cake |
| Baking Soda         | 1 tsp  | For cake |
| Cinnamon            | 1 tsp  | For cake |
| Butter              | ¼ lb   | For icing |
| Cocoa               | 3 Tbsp | For icing |
| Milk                | 6 Tbsp | For icing |
| Confectioners Sugar | 1 lb   | For icing |
| Vanilla             | 1 tsp  | For icing |
| Chopped Nuts        |        | Optional, for icing |

## Method

1. Mix flour and sugar together in a large bowl
1. In a saucepan, combine cocoa, ¼ lb butter, shortening, and 1 cup water — mix and bring to a boil, then pour over the flour and sugar and stir
1. Add 2 eggs, buttermilk, vanilla, baking soda, and cinnamon
1. Beat well and pour into a greased cookie sheet pan (about 10½×13×1 inch)
1. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes
1. For the icing, start 5 minutes before the cake is done: bring ¼ lb butter, 3 Tbsp cocoa, and 6 Tbsp milk to a boil
1. Remove from fire, add 1 lb confectioners sugar and beat until smooth
1. Add 1 tsp vanilla and chopped nuts if desired
1. Spread on the hot cake
1. Cut when cool
