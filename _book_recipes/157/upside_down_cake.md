# Upside Down Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butter           | 3 Tbsp | For topping |
| Brown Sugar      | 1 Cup  | For topping |
| Pineapple Slices |        | Or canned peaches |
| Cherries         |        | A few |
| Nuts             |        | A few |
| Brown Sugar      | ½ Cup  | For batter |
| Egg              | 1      | Well beaten |
| Salt             | ¼ tsp  | |
| Hot Milk         | ½ Cup  | |
| Cinnamon         | ½ tsp  | |
| Flour            | 1 Cup  | |
| Baking Powder    | 1½ tsp | |

## Method

1. Melt 3 Tbsp butter in a frying pan and add 1 cup brown sugar
1. Arrange pineapple slices (or canned peaches), a few cherries, and nuts over the sugar
1. For the batter, mix ½ cup brown sugar, beaten egg, salt, hot milk, and cinnamon
1. Add flour and baking powder and mix
1. Pour the batter over the fruit in the pan
1. Bake at 350°F for 30–40 minutes
1. Turn upside down onto a platter
1. Serve with whipped cream
