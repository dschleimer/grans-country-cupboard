# Frozen Raspberry Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Frozen Raspberries      | 10 oz  | |
| Sugar                   | 1 Cup  | |
| Egg Whites              | 2      | Room temperature |
| Lemon Juice             | 1 Tbsp | |
| Salt                    |        | Dash |
| Whipped Cream           | 1 Cup  | |
| Chopped Roasted Almonds | ¼ Cup  | |
| Baked Pie Shell         | 1      | 9-inch, with almond extract |

## Method

1. Add ½ tsp almond extract to pastry when making the pie shell, then bake and cool
1. Thaw the raspberries, reserving a few berries for garnish
1. Combine raspberries, sugar, egg whites, lemon juice, and salt
1. Beat for 15 minutes or until stiff
1. Fold in whipped cream and chopped almonds
1. Mound into the baked pie shell
1. Freeze until firm

## Modern Notes

* The long beating time (15 minutes) is essential — it aerates the mixture into a light, mousse-like filling
* Use a stand mixer for this, as beating by hand for 15 minutes would be exhausting
* This contains raw egg whites — use pasteurized eggs if food safety is a concern
