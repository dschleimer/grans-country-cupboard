# Fried Red Tomatoes + Milk Gravy

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------- | ------- | ----- |
| Margarine  | 1/2 lb  | Have extra on hand |
| Butter     | 1/2 lb  | Optional - May substitute for Margerine |
| Tomatoes   |         | Red |
| Flour      | 3 Tbsp  | Have extra on hand |
| Salt       |         | |
| Pepper     |         | |
| Onion Salt |         | |
| Sugar      |         | |
| Milk       | 1 quart | |

## Method

1. Wash, stem and halve tomatoes
1. Add 1/2 lb of Margerine to large, heavy skillet, adding more if needed
1. Pre-warm serving platter
1. Heat Margerine
1. Roll tomatoes in flour
1. Add tomatoes to hot margerine, with sides touching until skillet is full
1. Shake salt, pepper, and onion salt on tomatoes
1. Add a pinch of sugar to tomatoes
1. Brown tomatoes gently on one side
1. Turn tomatoes and brown other side
1. If tomatoes are too firm, cover with lid and cook on low heat for a few minutes
1. Using a spatula, remove firm tomatoes to warm serving platter, leaving softest tomatoes in pan
1. If more tomatoes are needed, add more Margerine and fry a second skilletful of tomatoes before proceeding
1. Stir ing 3 Tbsp of flour to sfot tomatoes and fat remaining in pan
1. Add 1 quart of milk while stirring constantly
1. Bring to a boil still stirring constantly
1. Add more salt and pepper to taste
1. Pour gravy over tomatoes and serve


## Notes
* We do not serve meat when we have this for dinner
* Good with Macaroni and Cheese
