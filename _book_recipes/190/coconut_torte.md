# Coconut Torte

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount | Notes |
| --------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Graham Cracker Crumbs | 1 Cup  | |
| Flaked Coconut        | ½ Cup  | Chopped |
| Nuts                  | ½ Cup  | |
| Egg Whites            | 4      | |
| Salt                  | ¼ tsp  | |
| Vanilla               | 1 tsp  | |
| Sugar                 | 1 Cup  | |
| Ice Cream             | 1 Pint | For serving |

## Method

1. Combine graham cracker crumbs, coconut, and nuts
1. Beat egg whites until soft peaks form, add salt and vanilla
1. Gradually add sugar, beating until stiff peaks form and sugar is dissolved
1. Fold the graham cracker mixture into the egg white mixture
1. Spread into a well-greased and floured 9-inch pie plate
1. Bake at 350°F for about 30 minutes
1. Cool completely
1. Cut in wedges and top with a scoop of ice cream
