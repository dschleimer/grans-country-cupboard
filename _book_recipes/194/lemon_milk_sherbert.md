# Lemon Milk Sherbert

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount | Notes |
| ----------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sugar       | 1 Cup  | |
| Lemon Juice | ½ Cup  | |
| Whole Milk  | 3 Cups | |

## Method

1. Combine milk with sugar and stir until dissolved
1. Add lemon juice — the milk will curdle slightly, this is normal
1. Freeze in an ice box tray
1. Stir periodically during freezing

## Notes From Gran

* Good for Sunday dinner or roast veal

## Modern Notes

* This is a very simple 3-ingredient sherbet — the acid from the lemon juice curdles the milk slightly, creating a tangy, refreshing frozen dessert -- Claude (AI)
* Stir every 30 minutes during freezing for a smoother texture -- Claude (AI)
* "Sherbert" with the extra R was a common spelling in Gran's era -- Claude (AI)
