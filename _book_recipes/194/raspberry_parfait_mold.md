# Raspberry Parfait Mold

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                 | Amount  | Notes |
| -------------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Raspberry Jello            | 1 pkg   | |
| Hot Raspberry Juice        | 1¼ Cups | And water |
| Vanilla Ice Cream          | 1 Pint  | |
| Drained Frozen Raspberries | 1 pkg   | reserve juice |
| Lady Fingers               | 6       | Split |
| Whipped Cream              |         | For topping |

## Method

1. Dissolve raspberry jello in hot raspberry juice and water
1. Stir in ice cream until melted
1. Chill until thickened but not set, stirring until it mounds
1. Fold in drained raspberries
1. Line sides of a mold with split lady fingers
1. Pour mixture into mold
1. Chill one hour or more
1. Serve with whipped cream on top
