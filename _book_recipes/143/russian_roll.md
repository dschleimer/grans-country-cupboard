# Russian Roll

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Gelatin             | 1 Tbsp | |
| Scalded Milk        | 2 Cups | |
| Egg Yolks           | 3      | Beaten |
| Sugar               | ½ Cup  | |
| Salt                | ¼ tsp  | |
| Vanilla             | 1 tsp  | |
| Whipping Cream      | 1 Cup  | |
| Angel Food Cake     | 1      | |
| Pecans              |        | For garnish |
| Maraschino Cherries |        | Cut up, for garnish |

## Method

1. Dissolve gelatin in cold water as directed on the package
1. Add hot scalded milk, salt, and sugar
1. Pour slowly over beaten egg yolks, stirring constantly
1. Cook gently until slightly thickened
1. Cool until the mixture begins to set
1. Whip the cream stiff and fold in along with vanilla
1. Split an angel food cake in half horizontally
1. Spread the cream mixture between the layers and on top
1. Garnish lightly with pecans and cut-up maraschino cherries
1. Refrigerate a couple of hours before serving

## Modern Notes

* This is a Bavarian cream filling used to fill and frost an angel food cake — a light, elegant dessert
* The gelatin sets the cream so it holds its shape when the cake is sliced
