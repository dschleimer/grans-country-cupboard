# Date and Nut Bread

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount          | Notes |
| ----------------- | --------------- | ----- |
| Dates             | 1 cup           | |
| Boiling Water     | 1½ cups         | |
| Beaten Light Eggs | 2               | |
| Sugar             | 1½ cups         | |
| Sifted Flour      | 2¼ cups + extra | |
| Baking Soda       | 2 tsp           | |
| Baking Powder     | ¼ tsp           | |
| Salt              | ½ tsp           | |
| Melted Butter     | 2 Tbsp          | |
| Vanilla           | 1 tsp           | |
| Nuts              | 1 cup           | Floured and Chopped |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 325 °F
1. Cut up dates
1. Boil 1½ cups water and cover dates in boiling water
1. Let dates soak for 10 minutes
1. Beat eggs until light
1. Add sugar to eggs and cream together
1. Mix together sifted flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt
1. Add dry ingredients together alternating to egg mixture alternating with liquid from dates
1. Melt butter and add to batter
1. Add vanilla
1. Fold in pieces of dates
1. Chop and flour nuts and fold in
1. Line pan with well greased parchment paper (see modern notes)
1. Bake 1 to 1¼ hours

## Modern Notes

* The original recipe calls for lining the pan with "well greased heavy wax paper" but wax paper, even heavy wax paper is not oven safe.  I transcribed as parchment paper since that serves the purpose and is actually intended for oven use
