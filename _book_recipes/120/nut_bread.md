# Nut Bread

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount         | Notes |
| ------------- | -------------- | ----- |
| Nuts          | 1 cup          | Chopped |
| Flour         | 4 cups + extra | |
| Sugar         | 1 cup          | |
| Milk          | 1½ cups        | |
| Baking powder | 4 tsp          | |
| Salt          | ¼ tsp          | |
| Egg           | 1              | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F
1. Mix together all ingredients
1. Grease and flour pan
1. Transfer dough to pan
1. Let rise in pan for 15 minutes
1. Bake for 40 minutes
