# Nut Bread

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Nuts | 1 cup | Chopped |
| Flour | 4 cups + extra | |
| Sugar | 1 cup | |
| Milk | 1 1/2 cups | |
| Baking powder | 4 tsp | |
| Salt | 1/4 tsp | |
| Egg | 1 | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350F
1. Mix together all ingredients
1. Grease and flour pan
1. Transfer dough to pan
1. Let rise in pan for 15 minutes
1. Bake for 40 minutes
