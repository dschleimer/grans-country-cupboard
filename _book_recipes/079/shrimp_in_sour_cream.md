# Shrimp in Sour Cream

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount | Notes |
| ------------ | ------ | ----- |
| Shrimp       | 1½ lbs | Peeled |
| Sour Cream   | 2 cups | |
| Sherry       | ¼ cup  | |
| Butter       | ½ cup  | Melted |
| Shallots     | 2      | Minced |
| Mushrooms    | ½ lb   | Sliced |
| Flour        | 2 Tbsp | |
| Salt         | 1 tsp  | |
| Black Pepper |        | Fresh Ground |

## Method

1. Peel shrimp
1. Melt butter in pan 
1. Sauté shrimp and Shallots in butter 5 minutes or until shrimp are pink
1. Add mushrooms and cook 5 minutes longer
1. Blend in flour, salt and pepper
1. Add sour cream gradually while constantly stirring
1. Cook, still constantly stirring, until thick
1. Remove from heat
1. Stir in sherry
