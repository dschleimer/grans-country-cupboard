# Toll House Cookies

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount  | Notes |
| --------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Butter          | 1 Cup   | |
| Brown Sugar     | ¾ Cup   | |
| Sugar           | ¾ Cup   | |
| Eggs            | 2       | |
| Flour           | 2¼ Cups | |
| Baking Soda     | 1 tsp   | |
| Salt            | ½ tsp   | |
| Chocolate Chips | 1 Cup   | |
| Vanilla         | 1 tsp   | |
| Water           | ½ tsp   | |
| Pecans          | 1 Cup   | Optional |

## Method

1. Cream butter, brown sugar, and sugar together
1. Beat in eggs
1. Sift flour, baking soda, and salt together and add
1. Stir in chocolate chips, vanilla, and water
1. Add pecans if desired
1. Drop on a greased cookie sheet, 1 tsp per cookie
1. Bake at 375°F for 10 minutes or until done
1. Do not overbake

## Modern Notes

* This is the classic Toll House chocolate chip cookie recipe, invented by Ruth Wakefield in the 1930s
* Do not overbake — they should be slightly underdone in the center as they continue to set while cooling
