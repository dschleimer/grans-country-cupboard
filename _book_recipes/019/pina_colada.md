# Pina Colada

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount   | Notes |
| ---------------- | -------- | ----- |
| Cream of Cocunut | 1 oz     | |
| Pineapple Juice  | 4 oz     | Unsweetened |
| White Rum        | 1 1/2 oz | |
| Ice              | 1 cup    | Crushed |

## Method

1. Mix for 10-20 seconds in blender
1. Serve over ice cubes in a tall glass

## Notes
* Recipe makes 1 drink
