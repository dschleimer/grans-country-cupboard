# Cheesecake Filling for 9 Inch Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Powdered Sugar          | ½ Cup  | |
| Milk                    | 2 Tbsp | |
| Vanilla                 | ½ tsp  | |
| Cream Cheese            | 3 oz   | |
| Sweetened Whipped Cream | 1 Cup  | |
| Graham Cracker Crust    | 1      | Or chocolate crumb crust |

## Method

1. Blend powdered sugar, milk, vanilla, and cream cheese together
1. Fold in the sweetened whipped cream
1. Pour into a graham cracker or chocolate crumb crust
1. Refrigerate until set

## Notes From Gran

* Frozen strawberries are good on top

## Modern Notes

* This is a no-bake cheesecake filling — quick and simple -- Claude (AI)
* The filling is quite light due to the whipped cream — more of a mousse-style cheesecake -- Claude (AI)
