# 7" Cheese Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount        | Notes |
| --------------- | ------------- | ----- |
| Graham crackers | 10            | crumbled |
| Butter          | ¼ lb          | |
| Nutmeg          | ⅛ tsp         | |
| Cinnamon        | ¼ tsp         | |
| Cream cheese    | 6 oz          | softened |
| Egg             | 1             | beaten |
| Sugar           | ¼ cup + 1 tsp | |
| Vanilla         | ½ tsp + 1 tsp | |
| Sour cream      | 1 cup         | |

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix cracker crumbs, butter, nutmeg, and cinnamon.
3. Press on bottom and sides of 7" pie pan to make crust.
4. To make filling, beat well cream cheese, egg, ¼ cup sugar, and ½ tsp vanilla.
5. Pour into crust and bake for 20 min.
6. Mix sour cream, 1 tsp sugar, and 1 tsp vanilla to make topping.
7. Cover pie with topping while hot.
8. Return to oven for 5 min.
9. Chill and serve.
