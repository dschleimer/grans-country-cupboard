# Italian Cheese Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount | Notes |
| ------------ | ------ | ----- |
| Cream Cheese | 16 oz   | two 8oz packages |
| Ricotta      | 1 lb  | May substitute Cottage Cheese - See modern notes|
| Cottage Cheese | 1 lb | Optional - may substitute for Ricotta - See modern notes|
| Sugar        | 1 1/2 Cups  | |
| Eggs         | 4      | |
| Lemon Juice  | 1½ sp  | |
| Vanilla      | 1 Tbsp | |
| Cornstarch   | 3 Tbsp | |
| Flour        | 3 Tbsp | |
| Butter       | ¼ lb   | |
| Sour Cream   | 1 pint | |
| Salt | 1 tsp | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 325°F
1. Cream the cream cheese and ricotta together until smooth
1. Add following ingredients in order given, creaming after each addition:
  1. Sugar
  1. Eggs one at a time
  1. lemon juice 
  1. vanilla
  1. cornstarch
  1. flour
  1. butter
  1. sour cream
  1. Salt
1. Pour into a springform pan (round, not tube)
1. Bake until set
1. Turn off the oven and let the cake remain inside until cool

## Modern Notes

* The original recipe calls for "1 lb cottage cheese - Ricotta".  I interpreted this as either being an option, but it may have refferred to what is sometimes called "small-curd cottage cheese" or "homemade ricotta" but appears to not actually be either of those things, as best I can tell.  See for example https://champagne-tastes.com/how-to-make-ricotta/  I would use real ricotta in this recipe, especially so given that it is described as *Italian*.  --David S
* Leaving the cheesecake in the turned-off oven to cool gradually helps prevent cracking -- Claude (AI)
* This is a classic Italian-American style cheesecake — the combination of ricotta and cream cheese gives it a lighter texture than an all-cream-cheese version -- Claude (AI)
