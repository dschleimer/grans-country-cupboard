# Beef Casserole

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                 | Amount      | Notes |
| -------------------------- | ----------- | ----- |
| Ground Beef                | 1 1/2 lbs   | |
| Green Peppers              | 1 lb        | Chopped |
| Onions                     | 1 lb        | Chopped |
| Celery                     | 6 ribs      | With leaves - Cut fine |
| Mushrooms                  | 8 oz        | Recipe specifies one 8 Oz can |
| Tomato Soup                | 1 can       | |
| Tomato Sauce               | 1 large can | |
| Stuffed Olives             | 1 small jar | |
| Pennsylvania Dutch Noodles | 1 lb        | Cooked |
| American Cheese            | 1/2 lb      | Grated |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350 F
1. Cook noodles and drain
1. Chop Green Peppers, and Onions
1. Finely cut up Celery
1. Brown meat slightly
1. Add Onions, peppers, and Celery
1. Steam until tender
1. Add mushrooms, tomato soup, tomato sauce, cooked Noodles, and Olives
1. Mix well and transfer to roasting pan
1. Grate cheese if necessary and put on top of roasting pan
1. Bake 15 to 20 minutes until cheese is light brown

## Notes
* Gran prefers to bake this 1 hour, then add the cheese and bake longer until cheese is light brown.
