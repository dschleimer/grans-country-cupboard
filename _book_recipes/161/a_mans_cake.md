# A Man's Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Shortening    | 1/2 cup | |
| Sugar         | 1 cup   | |
| Eggs          | 2       | separated |
| Salt          | 1/2 tsp | |
| Flour         | 2 cups  | |
| Baking powder | 2 tsp   | |
| Strong coffee | 1 cup   | cold |
| Nuts          | 3/4 cup | chopped |
| Vanilla       | 1 tsp   | |

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
2. Grease and flour a tube pan.
3. Cream shortening and sugar.
4. Add egg yolks.
5. Sift flour, salt, and baking powder together.
6. Alternately add dry ingredients and coffee to the wet mix.
7. Stir in chopped nuts and vanilla.
8. Beat egg whites until stiff.
9. Fold egg whites into batter.
10. Bake about one hour.
