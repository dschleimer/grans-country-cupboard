# Waffles

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Flour                   | 2 Cups | |
| Baking Powder           | 2 tsp  | |
| Salt                    | ½ tsp  | |
| Separated Eggs          | 3      |  |
| Milk                    | 1 Cup  | |
| Melted Butter           | 4 tsp  | |

## Method

1. Separate eggs whites and yolks
1. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt
1. Beat beaten egg yolks well
1. Melt butter
1. Combine egg yolks, butter, and milk
1. Mix wet ingredients into the dry ingredients
1. Beat egg whites stiff and fold into batter
1. Bake on a hot waffle iron

## Notes From Gran

* Makes 4 waffles

## Modern Notes

* Folding in the stiffly beaten egg whites separately is what makes these waffles light and crispy -- Claude (AI)
