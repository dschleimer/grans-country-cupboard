# Waffles

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Flour         | 2 Cups | |
| Baking Powder | 2 tsp  | |
| Salt          | ½ tsp  | |
| Egg Yolks     | 3      | Well beaten |
| Milk          | 1 Cup  | |
| Melted Butter | 4 tsp  | |
| Egg Whites    | 3      | Beaten stiff |

## Method

1. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt
1. Combine well-beaten egg yolks and milk
1. Add the melted butter
1. Mix wet ingredients into the dry ingredients
1. Fold in stiffly beaten egg whites
1. Bake on a hot waffle iron

## Notes From Gran

* Makes 4 waffles

## Modern Notes

* Folding in the stiffly beaten egg whites separately is what makes these waffles light and crispy
