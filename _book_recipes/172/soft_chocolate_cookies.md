# Soft Chocolate Cookies

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount    | Notes |
| ---------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Brown Sugar      | 1 Cup     | |
| Butter           | ¼ Cup     | |
| Egg              | 1         | Well beaten |
| Melted Chocolate | 2 Squares | |
| Milk             | ½ Cup     | |
| Flour            |           | Alternately with milk |
| Baking Soda      | ½ tsp     | In hot water, add to milk |
| Vanilla          | 1 tsp     | |
| Ground Nuts      | 1 Cup     | |

## Method

1. Cream brown sugar and butter together
1. Add 1 egg, well beaten, and 2 squares melted chocolate
1. Add milk alternately with flour
1. Dissolve baking soda in hot water and add to milk
1. Stir in ground nuts
1. Add vanilla and mix well
1. Bake at 400°F — do not overbake
1. Makes about 45 cookies
