# Evie's Goulash for 40 People

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount        | Notes |
| ----------------- | ------------- | ----- |
| Ground beef       | 8 lbs         | |
| Tomato Puree      | 4 large cans  | |
| Green Peppers     | 2 bags        | Chopped - Frozen |
| Onions            | 2 bags        | Chopped |
| Tomatoes          | 4 large cans  | Whole Tomoatoes |
| Creamettes        | 2 lbs         | This is a pasta brand - Cooked tender |
| Italian Seasoning |               | |
| Salt              |               | |
| Pepper            |               | |
| Sugar             | 1 small scoop | |
| Parmesan          | 1 lg          | Recipe calls for "1 lg Kraft Parmesan" |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 400 °F
1. Cook pasta to tender
1. Cut beef into lump size
1. Heat 3 ungreased frying pans **<u>hot</u>**
1. Split the beef across all 3 pans
1. Brown beef
1. Transfer beef to 2 large baking pans
1. Chop Onions and Green Peppers if not pre-chopped
1. Add tomatoes, tomato puree, green peppers, onions, pasta, italian seasoning, salt, and pepper
1. Sprinkle a **<u>small</u>** scoop of sugar on top
1. Mix up in the baking pans
1. Sprinkle parmesan on top
1. Do not stir
1. Bake at 400 °F until tender
1. Reduce to 300 °F and bake at least 2 hours more
1. If not wet when tested, add 1 cup boiling water

## Notes From Gran

* Serve with:
  * Coleslaw from 6 cabbages
  * 3 loaves of Italian Bread, sliced, buttered, and placed in paper bag
* This was served for Kiwanis luncheons
