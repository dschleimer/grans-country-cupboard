# Wrangler's Omelet with Cheese Sauce

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount     | Notes |
| --------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Butter          | 2 + 1 Tbsp | |
| Flour           | 2 Tbsp     | |
| Dry Mustard     | 1 tsp      | |
| Milk            | 1 cup      | |
| Cheddar Cheese  | 1 cup      | Shredded - Recipe calls for 1 cup (4 oz) shredded |
| Green Pepper    | ¼ cup      | Chopped |
| Pimento         | 2 Tbsp     | Chopped |
| Eggs            | 5          | Whites and yolks separated |
| Pepper          | Dash       | |
| Sour Cream      | ⅓ cup      | |
| Cream of Tartar | ½ tsp      | |
| Dried Beef      | 1 package  | Cut fine - 1 package is 3 or 4 oz, per recipe |

## Method

#### Cheese Sauce 
1. Shred cheese
1. Chop Green pepper and Pimento
1. Melt 1 Tbsp of Butter
1. Stir in Flour and Dry Mustard
1. Cook on low heat and blend well
1. Add milk
1. Bring to a boil for 1 minute while stirring
1. Add shredded cheese, stirring in until melted
1. Add green pepper and pimento
1. Keep sauce warm until ready to serve omelet.

#### Omelet
1. Preheat Oven to 325 °F
1. Separate Egg whites from yolks
1. Beat Egg Yolks with a dash pepper until brown colored
1. Fold in ½ cup sour cream to egg yolks
1. Beat egg whites with Cream of Tartar until stiff
1. Fold Yolks into whites
1. Fold in dried beef
1. Melt 2 Tbsp butter in 10 inch skillet
1. Turn egg mixture into skillet
1. Cook on low heat on stove top until puffy and browned on bottom
1. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until knife comes out clean
1. Cut into Wedges
1. Serve with cheese sauce
