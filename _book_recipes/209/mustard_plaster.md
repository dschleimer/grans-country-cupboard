# Mustard Plaster

<div style="background-color: #fee; border: 3px solid #c00; border-radius: 8px; padding: 1em 1.2em; margin: 1em 0;">
<strong style="color: #c00; font-size: 1.1em;">&#9888; Do Not Follow This Remedy</strong><br>
This historical home remedy is included only for completeness. <strong>Do not use mustard plasters</strong> — they can cause serious skin burns and blistering. If you or someone you know has a chest cold or congestion, please consult a doctor or see <a href="https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/common-cold/">NHS — Common Cold</a> for current medical guidance.
</div>

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount | Notes |
| --------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Dry Mustard     | 1 tsp  | |
| Ground Flaxseed | 3 Tbsp | |
| Lard            | 1 Tbsp | |
| Hot Water       |        | To mix |

## Method

1. Mix dry mustard, ground flaxseed, and lard
2. Add hot water to mix into a paste
3. Spread on a piece of old sheeting and lay on chest of person with bad cold or signs of congestion
4. Remove in a few minutes when skin gets slightly pink — otherwise skin will be burned
