# Shrimp Olive Spread

## Ingredients

| Ingredient             | Amount | Notes |
| ---------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Shrimp                 | 1 can  | Chopped. Recipe calls for a 5 oz can, though it appears to be sold as 6oz cans today |
| Lemon Rind             | ¾ tsp  | Grated |
| Mayonnaise             | ¼ cup  | |
| Pimento Stuffed Olives | ¼ cup  | Chopped |
| Dry Mustard            | ⅛ tsp  | |

## Method
1. Combine
1. Mix well
1. Chill

## Notes From Gran
* Spread on small party bread
* "1 cup" - presumably the amount this recipe makes
