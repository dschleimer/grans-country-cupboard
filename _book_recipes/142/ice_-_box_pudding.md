# Ice-Box Pudding

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sweet Chocolate         | ½ lb   | |
| Confectioners Sugar     | 4 Tbsp | |
| Evaporated Milk         | 4 Tbsp | Or heavy cream |
| Egg Yolks               | 3      | Well beaten |
| Beaten Stiff Egg Whites | 3      | |
| Lady Fingers            |        | Split |

## Method

1. Melt the sweet chocolate
1. Add confectioners sugar and evaporated milk (or heavy cream)
1. Remove from the stove
1. Add the well-beaten egg yolks and mix
1. Fold in the stiffly beaten egg whites
1. Pour over split lady fingers in a dish
1. Refrigerate for 24 hours
