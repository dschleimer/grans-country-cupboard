# Nutty Cheese Ball

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount   | Notes |
| -------------------- | -------- | ----- |
| Chopped Pecans       | 1.5 cups | Reserve 1.2 cup for rolling |
| Cream Cheese         | 24 Oz    | |
| Accent               | 1 Tbsp   | This is MSG, sold as [Ac'cent](https://accentflavor.com/) today |
| Worcestershire Sauce | 2 tbsp   | |
| Horseradish          | 1 tbsp   | Optional |

## Method
1. Let cheese and cream cheese come to room temperature
1. 1 cup chopped pecans and cheese.
1. Add Accent, Worcershire Sauce, and horseradish (if using) to mixture
1. Shape into one large ball
1. Roll in remaining 1/2 cup of chopped pecans
1. Chill until ready to serve
