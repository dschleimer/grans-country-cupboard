# Beans + Vienna Sausage

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                   | Amount         | Notes |
| ---------------------------- | -------------- | ----- |
| Green beans                  | 16 oz          | Cut - Canned - Drained |
| Kidney Beans                 | 16 oz          | Cut - Canned - Drained |
| Butter Beans                 | 16 oz          | Cut - Canned - Drained |
| Pork + Beans in Tomato Sauce | 16 oz          | Cut - Canned - Drained |
| Horseradish                  | 1 Tbsp         | |
| Salt                         | 1 tsp          | |
| Pepper                       | ¼ tsp          | |
| Vienna Sausage               | 10 oz (2 cans) | |
| Onion                        |                | Slices |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F
1. Uncan and drain the beans
1. Arrange beans in a 10 x 6 (inches) pan
1. Uncan sausage
1. Cut each sausage in half, lengthwise
1. Place sausages cut side down on top of beans
1. Bake for 30 minutes
1. When done, arrange onion slices on top, in a row down the middle of the pan
