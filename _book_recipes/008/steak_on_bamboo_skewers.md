# Steak on Bamboo Skewers

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount  | Notes |
| --------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Soy Sauce       | 1/2 cup | |
| Sherry          | 1/4 cup | |
| Garlic          | 1 Clove | |
| Ginger          | 1tbsp   | Powdered ginger per original recipe |
| Top round steak |         | Should be a thin rather than thick cut of steak |
| Bamboo Skewers  |         | |

## Method
1. Crush garlic
1. Mix soy sauce, sherry, garlic and ginger to make marinade
1. Cut steak into 1" strips
1. Marinade steak overnight in refrigerator
1. Put steak on bamboo skewers
1. Cook over open flames on a grill

## Notes

## Modern Notes
Original recipe says to "Barbeque on hibachi" which I believe is using Hibachi as a genericized trademark for a charcoal briquette grill.  I interpret this to mean any flame grill would work, and assume most readers will have a propane rather than charcoal briquette grill.  --David S
