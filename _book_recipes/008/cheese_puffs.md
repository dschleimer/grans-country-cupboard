# Cheese Puffs

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount | Notes |
| -------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sharp Grated Cheddar | 1/2 lb | |
| Butter               | 1/4 lb | |
| Flour                | 1 cup  | |
| Salt                 | 1 tsp  | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
1. Cream butter
1. Add other ingredients
1. Roll into small balls about 1/2" diameter
1. Bake 10 minutes

## Notes
*  Makes approximately 36 balls
*  May be frozen
