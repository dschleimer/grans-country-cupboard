# Green Tomato Relish or Pickle

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount | Notes |
| -------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Green Tomatoes | 1 Peck | Cut |
| Onions         | 6 lbs  | Fine |
| Salt           | 1 Cup  | For overnight |
| Sugar          | 2 Cups | |
| Cloves         | 1 Cup  | |
| Dry Mustard    | 2 Tbsp | |
| Mustard Seed   | 2 Tbsp | |
| Celery Seed    | 2 Tbsp | |
| Vinegar        | 2 Cups | |

## Method

1. Cut green tomatoes and onions fine
1. Sprinkle with 1 cup salt overnight
1. Drain well in the morning
1. Add sugar, cloves, dry mustard, mustard seed, and celery seed
1. Cover with 2 cups vinegar
1. Boil 2 hours
1. Place in a stone crock with a cover — could also be put in jars
