# Hot Milk + Whipped Cream Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient             | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Eggs                   | 4       | |
| Sugar                  | 1½ Cups | |
| Hot Milk               | ¾ Cup   | |
| Flour                  | 1½ Cups | |
| Butter                 | 2 Tbsp  | |
| Salt                   |         | Pinch |
| Vanilla                | 1 tsp   | |
| Baking Powder          | 4 tsp   | |
| Whipping Cream         | 1 pint  | For frosting |
| Egg                    | 1       | Well beaten, for frosting |
| German Sweet Chocolate | ½ bar   | Melted and cooled, for frosting |

## Method

1. Beat eggs well and add sugar, beating again
1. Sift flour, salt, and baking powder together and add
1. Heat milk with butter and add to the mixture
1. Add vanilla
1. Pour into 2 greased and floured 9-inch layer pans
1. Bake at 325°F for about 20 minutes
1. Cool completely
1. For the frosting, whip the cream, add the well-beaten egg, and fold in the melted and cooled chocolate
1. Spread thickly between layers and on top of the cake
1. Keep refrigerated

## Modern Notes

* The frosting contains a raw egg — use pasteurized eggs if food safety is a concern
* German sweet chocolate is a mild, sweetened baking chocolate (Baker's German's Sweet Chocolate is the classic brand)
