# Cheese Soufflée

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount     | Notes |
| ------------ | ---------- | ----- |
| Butter       | 3 Tbsp     | |
| Flour        | 4 Tbsp     | White or Whole Wheat |
| Milk         | 1 1/4 cups | |
| Sharp Cheese | 3/4 cups   | Grated |
| Eggs         | 3          | Whites and yolks separated |
| Paprika      | Dash       | Use Paprika or Red Pepper, not both |
| Red Pepper   | Dash       | Use Paprika or Red Pepper, not both |
| Salt         | Pinch      | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 375F
1. Butter mold
1. Separate eggs whites from yolks
1. Grate Cheese
1. Mix butter, flour, and milk
1. Cook to a cream sauce
1. Add cheese
1. Add Egg Yolks one at a time
1. Add Salt and Papprika or Red Pepper
1. Beat Egg whites stiff and fold in
1. Transfer to butter mold
1. Put mold in oven over water
1. Bake 20 minutes or until done
