# for Dried Beef or Sugar Cured Hams

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount  | Notes |
| ----------- | ------- | ----- |
| Meat        | 100 lbs | |
| Fine Salt   | 5 lbs   | |
| Brown Sugar | 4 oz    | |
| Saltpeter   | 2 oz    | |

## Method

1. For each 100 lbs of meat: mix 5 lbs fine salt, 4 oz brown sugar, and 2 oz saltpeter
2. Mix thoroughly — put through colander
3. Put meat skin side down after grinding salt cure over it
4. Be sure to fill into all holes as well as possible — also cover about ¼" thick or as thick as possible
5. Re-cover in 24 hours — these will be cured in 2 weeks
6. Hang pieces up to dry in cool, dry room

## Modern Notes
