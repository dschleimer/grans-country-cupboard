# No-Cooking Hard Sauce

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Confectioners Sugar | 1 Cup  | |
| Butter              | ½ Cup  | |
| Boiling Water       | 2 tsp  | |
| Vanilla             | 1 tsp  | Or rum |

## Method

1. Cream the butter well
1. Add confectioners sugar gradually, beating until smooth
1. Add vanilla (or rum)
1. Add the boiling water carefully and beat until light
1. Set in a cool place until ready to use

## Modern Notes

* Hard sauce is a traditional accompaniment for steamed puddings, gingerbread, and fruit desserts
* It is served cold and melts on contact with warm desserts — essentially a sweet butter sauce
* The name "hard" refers to its firm, butter-like consistency when chilled
