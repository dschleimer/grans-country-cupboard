# Oyster Cakes Hollandaise

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount                          | Notes |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- | ----- |
| Onions            | 1 Tbsp                          | Finely chopped |
| Butter            | 2 + 2 Tbsp                      | |
| Eggs              | 2                               | Beaten |
| Bread Crumbs      | 3 cups                          | Soft - Crumble 4 slices soft bread |
| Oysters           | 1 pint                          | Drained - Chopped - 2 cups after chopping |
| Celery            | ½ cup                           | Finely chopped |
| Milk              | ¼ cup                           | |
| Parsley           | 2 Tbsp snipped OR 1 Tbsp flakes | |
| Lemon Juice       | 1 Tbsp                          | |
| Bread Crumbs      | ¾ cup                           | Fine dry crumbs |
| Salt              | ¼ tsp                           | |
| Paprika           | ½ tsp                           | |
| Hollandaise sauce |                                 | |

## Method

1. Finely chop onion and celery
1. Snip parsley if using fresh
1. Crumble soft bread
1. Drain and chop Oysters
1. Saute onion in 2 Tbsp butter
1. Beat eggs and combine with onion, soft bread crumbs, oysters, celery, milk, parsley, and lemon juice
1. Chill 2 hours
1. Shape in 8 cakes ½ inch thick
1. Coat with Dry Bread crumbs, salt, and paprika
1. Melt 2 Tbsp Butter in skillet
1. Brown cakes
1. Reduce heat and cook slowly 6-8 minutes
1. Prepare Hollandaise sauce by directions on package
1. Serve Hollandaise over oyster cakes
