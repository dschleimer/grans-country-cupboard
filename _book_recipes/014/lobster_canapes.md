# Lobster Canapes

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount | Notes |
| ------------ | ------ | ----- |
| Lobster Meat | ¾ lb   | Cooked and Ground |
| Onion        | 2 Tbsp | Minced |
| Eggs         | 2      | Beaten |
| Salt         | ½ tsp  | |
| Black Pepper |        | Freshly Ground |
| Corn Meal    | ¾ cup  | Yellow Corn Meal |

## Method
1. Heat Cooking oil to 375 °F in deep fryer or in a large pot on stovetop
1. Cook and grind lobster if making from fresh
1. Mince onion
1. Beat eggs
1. Combine all ingredients and mix well
1. Shape into balls
1. Roll in corn meal
1. Fry in a basket for 3 minutes
1. Drain

## Notes From Gran
* Makes 48 balls
