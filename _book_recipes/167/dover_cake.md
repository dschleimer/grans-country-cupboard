# Dover Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butter        | ½ lb   | |
| Sugar         | 1 lb   | |
| Eggs          | 6      | Add one at a time |
| Flour         | 3 Cups | |
| Baking Powder | ¼ tsp  | |
| Salt          |        | Pinch |
| Cold Water    | ½ Cup  | |
| Vanilla       | 1 tsp  | |

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar together
1. Add eggs one at a time, beating after each
1. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together
1. Add dry ingredients alternately with cold water
1. Stir in vanilla
1. Pour into a greased and floured tube pan
1. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour or until done
