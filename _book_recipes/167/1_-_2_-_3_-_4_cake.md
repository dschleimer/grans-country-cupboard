# 1 - 2 - 3 - 4 Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Butter        | 1 cup   | |
| Sugar         | 2 cups  | |
| Eggs          | 4       | |
| Milk          | 1 cup   | |
| Flour         | 3 cups  | |
| Baking Powder | 2 tsps  | |
| Salt          | 1/2 tsp | |
| Vanilla       | 1 tsp   | |


## Method
1. Preheat oven to 325.
2. Grease and flour a 10-inch tube pan.
3. Separate egg whites from yolks.
4. Cream butter and sugar.
5. Drop in yolks and beat. Set aside.
6. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt.
7. Alternately add milk and flour to the wet mixture.
8. Add vanilla.
9. Beat until smooth.
10. Beat egg whites until stiff.
11. Fold in egg whites.
12. Bake for one hour or until done.
## Notes
Favorite for birthdays with soft white or chocolate frosting
