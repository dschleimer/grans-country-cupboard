# Ice Cream

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Eggs          | 2       | |
| Sugar         | 1 Cup   | |
| Flour         | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Boiling Milk  | 1 Pint  | Generous |
| Cream         | 1 Quart | |
| Crushed Fruit | 1 Cup   | Peaches or strawberries |
| Sugar         | 1 Cup   | For fruit |

## Method

1. Beat eggs, sugar, and flour together
1. Stir into a generous pint of boiling milk
1. Cool the custard completely
1. Add cream and crushed fruit sweetened with 1 cup sugar
1. Put in an ice cream freezer
1. Freeze until heavy and creamy
1. Remove the paddle and freeze solid
