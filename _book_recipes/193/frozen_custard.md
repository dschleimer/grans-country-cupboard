# Frozen Custard

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------- | ------- | ----- |
| Milk       | 1 Quart | |
| Cream      | 1 Quart | |
| Sugar      | 2 Cups  | |
| Eggs       | 6       | |
| Vanilla    |         | To taste |

## Method

1. Beat eggs and sugar until light and lemon-colored
1. Scald the milk
1. Add the sugar slowly to the scalded milk
1. Add the egg mixture, stirring constantly
1. Put mixture into a pan and cook slowly to a soft custard, stirring all the time, until it coats the back of a spoon
1. Cool the custard completely before freezing
1. Stir in the cream and beat well
1. Add vanilla to taste
1. Freeze in an ice cream freezer
1. Makes a little more than 3 quarts

## Notes From Gran

* 1935

## Modern Notes

* This is a rich egg custard frozen dessert — 6 eggs and a quart of cream make it very luxurious
* The custard must coat the back of a spoon (about 170°F) — do not let it boil or the eggs will curdle
* Cool completely before adding cream and freezing
