# Lemon Cream Filling - for Cakes

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sugar         | 1 Cup  | |
| Flour         | 5 Tbsp | |
| Egg           | 1      | Slightly beaten |
| Lemon Juice   | ⅓ Cup  | |
| Water         | ⅔ Cup  | |
| Butter        | 2 tsp  | |
| Lemon Rind    |        | Grated, optional |
| Whipped Cream | ½ Cup  | For spreading |

## Method

1. Combine sugar and flour in a double boiler
1. Add slightly beaten egg, lemon juice, and water
1. Mix well and place over boiling water
1. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring constantly
1. Chill, then fold in grated lemon rind
1. Add ½ cup whipped cream and use as filling to spread between layers of cake
1. For the other half, fold in ¾ cup whipped cream and use as sauce
