# Raised Dinner Rolls

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Milk              | 1 Cup  | Scalded |
| Cake Yeast        | 1      | |
| Flour             | 3 Cups | |
| Sugar             | 3 Tbsp | |
| Salt              | ½ tsp  | |
| Melted Shortening | ¼ Cup  | |
| Eggs              | 2      | |
| Butter            |        | For brushing |

## Method

1. Scald the milk and cool to lukewarm
1. Add yeast and stir until dissolved
1. Add 1 cup of flour and beat until smooth
1. Let rise until light and full of bubbles
1. Add sugar, salt, melted shortening, and beaten eggs
1. Add the remaining flour and mix all together
1. Let the dough rise again
1. Shape into small rolls and place in a greased pan
1. Let rise again before baking
1. Bake at 425°F for 10–15 minutes
1. Remove from oven and brush with butter
