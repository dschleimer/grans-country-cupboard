# Raised Dinner Rolls

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Scalded Milk      | 1 Cup  | |
| Cake Yeast        | 1      | |
| Flour             | 1 + 2 Cups | |
| Sugar             | 1 1/2 Tbsp | |
| Salt              | ½ tsp  | |
| Melted Shortening | ¼ Cup  | |
| Butter            |        | For brushing |

## Method

1. Scald the milk and cool until merely warm
1. Crumble yeast cake into warm milk
1. Add 1 cup of flour and beat until smooth
1. Let rise until light and full of bubbles
1. Add sugar, salt, melted shortening, and enough flour to knead.  Mix all together
1. Form into small rolls
1. Let the rolls rise again in a warm place
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
1. Bake for 10–15 minutes
1. Remove from oven and brush with butter

## Modern Notes
* The ingredient list calls for 3 cups of flour, but the original method calls for 1 cup of flour at first, and then "enough flour to knead" later.  I interpret the latter as indicating you add flour while kneading until it reaches the right texture, and 3 is the maximum total amount of flour you're expected to need.  --David S
* One cake of compressed yeast can be substituted with 1 packet (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast -- Claude (AI)
* "Scald" milk means heating it to just below boiling — about 180°F — then cooling it before adding yeast -- Claude (AI)
