# Italian Bread Sticks

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Warm Water       | ⅔ Cup   | |
| Active Dry Yeast | 1 pkg   | |
| Salt             | 1 tsp   | |
| Sugar            | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Soft Shortening  | ¼ Cup   | |
| Flour            | 2½ Cups | |
| Egg              | 1       | For egg wash |
| Water            | 1 Tbsp  | For egg wash |
| Sesame Seeds     |         | Or celery/poppy seeds or coarse salt |

## Method

1. In a mixing bowl, dissolve yeast in warm water
1. Add salt, sugar, shortening, and half the flour and stir well
1. Add the rest of the flour and mix until combined
1. Knead on a floured board until smooth, about 5 minutes
1. Cover and let rise for 1 hour or until doubled in bulk
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
1. Divide dough in half, then cut each half into 16 pieces
1. Roll each piece into a bread stick shape, 6 to 10 inches long
1. Place on a greased baking sheet, spaced apart
1. Brush with beaten egg mixed with 1 Tbsp water
1. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, celery seeds, poppy seeds, or coarse salt
1. Bake at 400°F for 20–25 minutes until golden brown

## Notes From Gran

* Makes 48 bread sticks

## Modern Notes

* One package of active dry yeast is equivalent to 2¼ tsp
* "Soft shortening" means shortening at room temperature — Crisco or similar vegetable shortening
