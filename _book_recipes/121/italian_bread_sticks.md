# Italian Bread Sticks

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Warm Water       | ⅔ Cup   | |
| Active Dry Yeast | 1 pkg   | |
| Salt             | 1 tsp   | |
| Sugar            | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Soft Shortening  | ¼ Cup   | |
| Flour            | 2 Cups Divided + Extra | |
| Egg              | 1       | For egg wash |
| Water            | 1 Tbsp  | For egg wash |
| Sesame Seeds     |         | Optional |
| Celery Seeds     |         | Optional |
| Poppy Seeds      |         | Optional |
| Corn Meal        |         | Optional |
| Coarse Salt      |         | Optional |


## Method

1. In a mixing bowl, dissolve yeast in warm water
1. Add salt, sugar, shortening, and half the flour
1. Beat hard until smooth
1. Mix in the rest of the flour
1. Knead on a floured board until smooth, about 5 minutes
1. Cover and let rise for 1 hour or until doubled in size
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
1. Grease baking sheet
1. Divide dough in half, then cut each half into 24 pieces
1. Roll each piece into a bread stick shape, 6 to 10 inches long
1. Place on greased baking sheet, 1 inch apart
1. Beat egg together with 1 tbps of water to make egg wash, and brush over breadsticks
1. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, celery seeds, poppy seeds, corn meal, or coarse salt
1. Bake 20–25 minutes until golden brown

## Notes From Gran

* Makes 48 bread sticks

## Modern Notes

* One package of active dry yeast is equivalent to 2¼ tsp -- Claude (AI)
* "Soft shortening" means shortening at room temperature — Crisco or similar vegetable shortening -- Claude (AI)
