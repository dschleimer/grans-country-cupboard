# Cherry Jubilee Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                  | Amount | Notes |
| --------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butter                      | ½ Cup + Extra  |  |
| Sugar                       | 3 Tbsp |  |
| Egg Yolk                    | 1      |  |
| Flour                       | 1 Cup  |  |
| Flaked Coconut | 1 cup | |
| Pitted Black Cherries | 1 can  | |
| Water | | |
| Black Cherry Gelatin        | 1 pkg  | 3 oz |
| California Port Wine        | ½ Cup  | |
| Salt                        | ⅛ tsp  | |
| Vanilla Ice Cream           | 1 pint | |
| Whipped Cream               |        | For serving |
| While Cherries | | For garnish |

## Method

1. Butter 9 inch pie pan
1. Cream the butter with sugar and egg yolk
1. Blend in 1 cup flour
1. Stir in 1 cup flaked coconut
1. Press the mixture into pie pan 
1. Chill for 30 minutes
1. Preheat oven to 350°F
1. Bake atfor 25 minutes or until lightly browned
1. cool completely
1. Drain cherries and measure the juice — add enough water to make 2/3 cup total of liquid
1. Heat the liquid to boiling and dissolve the gelatin in it
1. Stir in the port wine, salt
1. Drop in ice cream a tablespoon at a time, letting it melt + combine
1. Chill until slightly thickened
1. Stir in the drained cherries
1. Pour into the baked crust
1. Chill until set
1. Garnish with whipped cream and whole cherries
1. Serve

## Modern Notes

* This is a gelatin-set pie that uses melted ice cream as part of the filling — a popular technique in mid-century desserts -- Claude (AI)
* California port wine gives this its "jubilee" character — you could substitute cherry juice for a non-alcoholic version -- Claude (AI)
* The cookie-style crust (butter, sugar, egg yolk, flour) is a pâte sucrée rather than a traditional pie crust -- Claude (AI)
