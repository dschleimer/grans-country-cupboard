# Seafood Salad Mold for 16

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount      | Notes |
| -------------------- | ----------- | ----- |
| Unflavored Gelatin   | 6 envelopes | |
| Tomato Juice         | 6 cups      | Divided |
| Lemon Juice          | ⅔ cups      | |
| Salt                 | 1½ tsp      | |
| Worcestershire Sauce | 1 Tbsp      | |
| Horseradish          | ⅓ cup       | |
| Mayonnaise           | 3 cups      | |
| Cooked Seafood       | 6 cups      | Shrimp, Crab, or Lobster |
| Celery               | 3 cups      | Chopped |

## Method

1. Chop celery
1. Place 3 cups tomato juice into a deep saucepan
1. Sprinkle Gelatin over tomato juice in saucepan
1. Place saucepan over moderate heat
1. Stir in remaining Tomato Juice, and all ingredients other than Seafood and Celery
1. Beat until smooth
1. Chill and stir occasionally until mixture mounds when dropped from spoon
1. Add seafood and celery
1. Turn into two 8 cup molds
1. Chill until firm
1. Unmold
