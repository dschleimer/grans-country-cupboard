# North Carolina Country-Style Ham

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount | Notes |
| ----------- | ------ | ----- |
| Ham         |        | Country-Style |
| Cloves      |        | Whole |
| Brown Sugar |        | |

## Method

1. Thoroughly scrub and rinse Ham
1. Place in large pot
1. Completely cover with water
1. Soak 24 hours
1. Drain
1. Cover with cold water
1. Simmer (not boil) 25 minutes per pound
1. Drain, preserving cooking water if desired (see note)
1. Preheat oven to 350 °F
1. Remove all skin
1. Trim fat
1. Place whole Cloves in fat side, about 1 inch apart
1. Sprinkle with brown sugar
1. Bake about ½ hour
1. Slice thinly
1. Serve Hot or Cold
1. Keep refrigerated until all is used


## Notes From Gran

* Cooking water (from simmering the ham) may be used for cooking vegetables or soups
