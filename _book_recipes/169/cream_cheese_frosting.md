# Cream Cheese Frosting

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount | Notes |
| -------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Cream Cheese   | 1      | 8 oz pkg |
| Butter         | ¼ lb   | |
| Powdered Sugar | 1 Box  | 1 lb |
| Vanilla        | 1 tsp  | |

## Method

1. Beat cream cheese and butter together until smooth
1. Add powdered sugar and vanilla
1. Beat until smooth and spreadable
1. Spread on cake
