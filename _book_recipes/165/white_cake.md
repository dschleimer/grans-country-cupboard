# White Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Sugar         | 1½ Cups | |
| Butter        | ½ Cup   | |
| Milk          | 1 Cup   | |
| Flour         | 2 Cups  | |
| Baking Powder | 2 tsp   | |
| Salt          | ¼ tsp   | |
| Vanilla       | 1 tsp   | |
| Egg Whites    | 5       | Stiffly beaten |

## Method

1. Cream sugar and butter together
1. Add milk and mix well
1. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together
1. Add dry ingredients to the creamed mixture
1. Stir in vanilla
1. Fold in stiffly beaten egg whites
1. Pour into a greased and floured tube pan
1. Bake at 300°F until done
