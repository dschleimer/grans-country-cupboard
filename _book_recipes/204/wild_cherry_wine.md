# Wild Cherry Wine

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount | Notes |
| -------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Wild Cherries  |        | Ripe |
| Sugar          | 1½ lbs | Per gallon of juice |
| Lukewarm Water |        | For diluting |

## Method

1. Put cherries in — ferment for 1 week, stirring now and then
2. Crush and press out pure juice
3. To 1 part pure juice, prepare brine by dissolving 1½ lbs sugar to each gallon of lukewarm water
4. Place in barrel by alternating one gallon of juice with two gallons of sweetened water
5. Keep barrel filled to overflowing — continue filling with sweetened water for a few weeks
6. Keep bung open until fermentation has ceased — then seal for a year before using
