# Cruellers

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Eggs                | 2      | |
| Sugar               | 1 Cup  | |
| Crisco Oil          | ⅓ Cup  | |
| Milk                | 1 Cup  | |
| Sifted Flour        | 4 Cups | |
| Baking Powder       | 4 tsp  | |
| Salt                | ½ tsp  | |
| Vanilla             | ½ tsp  | |
| Confectioners Sugar |        | For sprinkling |

## Method

1. Combine beaten eggs, sugar, and oil
1. Add milk, then dry ingredients — flour, baking powder, and salt
1. Stir only until smooth
1. Rest dough for 5–10 minutes
1. Roll out on a lightly floured board, cut into strips, and twist
1. Fry doughnuts at 350°F for 1–2 minutes per side, until golden brown, turning once with a fork
1. Remove from pan and drain on paper towels
1. Sprinkle with confectioners sugar before serving

## Modern Notes

* Cruellers (also spelled crullers) are twisted fried dough — lighter than a doughnut -- Claude (AI)
* Oil temperature is important — too hot and they brown before cooking through, too cool and they absorb excess oil -- Claude (AI)
* Drain well on paper towels and dust generously with confectioners sugar while still warm -- Claude (AI)
