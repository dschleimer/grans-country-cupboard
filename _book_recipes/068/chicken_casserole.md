# Chicken Casserole

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount     | Notes |
| --------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Noodles         | 1 Package  | Cooked |
| Chicken Breasts | 3          | Boneless - Skinless - Cooked - Cut bite size |
| Celery          | 1 cup      | Cut fine |
| Cheese Soup     | 1 can      | |
| Celery Soup     | 1 can      | Use Celery *OR* Mushroom soup, not both |
| Mushroom Soup   | 1 can      | Use Celery *OR* Mushroom soup, not both |
| Cheddar Cheese  | 1 cup      | Grated |
| Milk            | 1 1/2 cups | To thin |
| Salt            | To Taste   | |
| Pepper          | To Taste   | |

## Method

1. Cook noodles and drain
1. If needed, debone, deskin, cook and cut chicken breasts into bite-sized pieces
1. Cut celery fine
1. Grease baking dish
1. Combine noodles, chicken, Celery, soups and cheese
1. Add milk to thin - about 1 1/2 cups
1. Mix together and put in greased baking dish
1. Set in refrigerator overnight
1. Add more milk if needed before baking
1. Preheat oven to 350F
1. Add salt and pepper to tast
1. Bake about 1 hour

## Notes
* A few buttered crumbs on top is very good
