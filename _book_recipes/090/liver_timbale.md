# Liver Timbale

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Chicken Livers | 1/2 lb | |
| Cream | 2/3 cup | Whipped after measuring |
| Eggs | 2 | Slightly beaten |
| Onion | 1 | |
| Salt | To Taste | |
| Pepper | To Taste | |
| Butter |  | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 325F
1. Whip cream
1. Cook onion in butter until soft
1. Add liver
1. Saute until done
1. Mash through a sieve
1. Lightly beat eggs and mix in
1. Add whipped cream
1. Grease small mold
1. Pour mixtur einto mold
1. Bake over water bath (like a custard) for 30 minutes
1. Test with tooth pick
1. Let stand 10 minutes before unmolding
1. Serve Cold

## Notes From Gran

* Serve sliced on crackers or rye bread
