# Rich White Birthday Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount  | Notes |
| -------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Butter         | 1 Cup   | |
| Sugar          | 3 Cups  | |
| Flour          | 3 Cups  | |
| Baking Powder  | 1 tsp   | |
| Salt           | 1 tsp   | |
| Half and Half  | 1⅓ Cups | |
| Vanilla        | 2 tsp   | |
| Almond Extract | ¼ tsp   | |
| Egg Whites     | 6       | Beaten stiff |

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar together
1. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together
1. Add dry ingredients to the butter mixture alternately with the half and half
1. Add vanilla and almond extract
1. Fold in stiffly beaten egg whites
1. Pour into 3 greased and floured layer pans
1. Bake at 350°F for about 25–30 minutes
1. Ice to taste

## Modern Notes

* This is a classic white layer cake — egg whites only (no yolks) keep it pure white
* The half and half makes it richer than a cake made with regular milk
