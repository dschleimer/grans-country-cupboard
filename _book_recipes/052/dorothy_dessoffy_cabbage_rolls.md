# Dorothy Dessoffy's Cabbage Rolls

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount       | Notes |
| ----------- | ------------ | ----- |
| Cabbage     | 2 heads      | |
| Ground Beef | 5 lbs        | |
| Ground Pork | 3 lbs        | |
| Rice        | 1 lbs        | Uncooked |
| Salt        |              | |
| Pepper      |              | |
| Onions      | 6 large      | |
| Sauerkraut  | 2 large cans | |
| Pork Loin   | 2            | |
| Tomatoes    | 2 large cans | |
| Flour       |              | For Gravy |
| Sour Cream  |              | At serving time |

## Method

1. Add whole heads of cabbage to already boiling water
1. While cabbage cooks, mix together Ground Beef, Ground Pork, Rice, Onions, Salt and Pepper
1. Pull cabbage out leaf by leaf
1. Slice thin *ribs* of cabbage
1. Fill each cabbage rib with meat mixture and roll up
1. Rinse Sauerkraut
1. Put Sauerkraut around edge of large kettle
1. Put pork loin in middle of kettle
1. Put rolled up cabbage on top of loin and Sauerkraut
1. Put canned tomatoes on top on cabbge rolls
1. Cover with water
1. Cook at least 4 hours on top of stove
1. Refirigerate over night
1. Cook 4 hours in morning
1. Serve in 2 bowls:
  * Pork in one bowl
  * Sauerkraut in other bowl
1. Thicken the juices from the kettles with flour to make a gravy
1. Add sour cream to gravy and add to Sauerkraut bowl
1. Serve

## Notes From Gran
* Use 2 big kettles
* Original recipe said "You *probably* need 2 heads of cabbage" - emphasis added
