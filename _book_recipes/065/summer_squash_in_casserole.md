# Summer Squash in Casserole

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount    | Notes |
| -------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Squash         | 5 medium  | Yellow, white or Zuccini |
| Onion          | 1/2 cup   | Chopped |
| Butter         | 2 Tbsp    | Melted - More for Casserole dish |
| Salt           | 1 1/2 tsp | |
| Pepper         | To Taste  | |
| Cheddar Cheese | 1/2 cup   | Grated |
| Fried Onions   | 1 can     | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 325F
1. Butter Casserole dish
1. Wash squash
1. Cut squash into chunks and place in dish
1. Chop Onions and add to dish
1. Melt butter and add to dish
1. Add Salt and Pepper
1. Grate Cheese on top
1. Bake 30 minutes or until Squash is tender.
1. Remove from stove
1. Sprinkle Onions on top
