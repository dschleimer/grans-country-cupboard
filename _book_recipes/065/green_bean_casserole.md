# Green Bean Casserole

## Ingredients

| Ingredient             | Amount | Notes |
| ---------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Green Beans            | 2 cups | Canned - Preferably French Style |
| Cream of Mushroom Soup | 1 can  | Undiluted |
| Fried Onion            | 1 can  | |
| Butter                 | 1/8 lb | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350F
1. Mix beans and mushrooms in Casserole dish
1. Put onions on top
1. Dot with butter
1. Bake 20 minutes
