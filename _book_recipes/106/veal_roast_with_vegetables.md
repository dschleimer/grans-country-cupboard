# Veal Roast with Vegetables

## Ingredients

| Ingredient               | Amount      | Notes |
| ------------------------ | ----------- | ----- |
| Veal Shoulder            | 4-5 lbs     | Boned and Rolled |
| Margarine                | 2 Tbsp      | |
| Onion Soup Mix           | 1 envelope  | Lipton's |
| Apple Juice              | 1 cup       | |
| White Onions                   | 1 bag       | Frozen  |
| Quartered White Potatoes | 4           | |
| Carrots                  | 4 large     | In Pieces |
| Sliced Parsnips          | 2 medium    | |
| Sliced Mushrooms         | 1 large can | |
| Flour                    | 3 Tbsp      | |

## Method

1. Have the butcher debone and roll a veal shoulder
1. Brown Veal in margerine on stovetop
1. Add Onion soup mix and apple juice
1. Heat to boiling
1. Cover and simmer for 2 hours - turning meat twice
1. Add onions
1. Simmer 30 additional minutes
1. Quarter potatoes, cut up carrots, slice parsnips and add
1. Simmer 30 minutes or until meat and vegetables are tender
1. Remove meat and carve into ¼ inch slices
1. Add sliced mushrooms to vegetables
1. Thicken vegetables with flour
1. Stir constantly until thick and boil for 1 minute
1. Place meat slices back over vegetables
