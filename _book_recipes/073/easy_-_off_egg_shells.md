# Easy - Off Egg Shells

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Eggs       |        | |

## Method

1. Have eggs at room temperature
1. Cover with cold water
1. Cover pot with lid
1. Let come to gentle boil
1. Boil for 10 minutes
1. Turn off heat and allow to cool in own water
1. Peel easily
