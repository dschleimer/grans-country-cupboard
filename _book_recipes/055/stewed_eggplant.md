# Stewed Eggplant

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount      | Notes |
| ---------- | ----------- | ----- |
| Eggplant   | 1           | Pared - Sliced thin |
| Salt       |             | |
| Pepper     |             | |
| Cream      | 1 cup       | |
| Butter     | large piece | |
| Flour      | a little    | |

## Method

1. Pare eggplant and slice it thin
1. Add Salt and Pepper to taste
1. Cover with water and boil for 1 hour, stirring frequently
1. Drain well
1. Add cream, a large piece of butter
1. Bring to a boil
1. When it reaches a boil, sift in a little flour to thicken

## Notes From Gran
* Do not cook in tin or iron as it will darken
* "Like my mother made it" -- Grandad Marter
