# Chestnuts in the Shell

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount | Notes |
| ----------- | ------ | ----- |
| Chestnuts   |        | |
| Baking Soda | ¼ tsp  | For dried chestnuts |

## Method

1. Cut a cross with a sharp knife on the flat side of each chestnut
1. Place in a saucepan and cover with water
1. Bring to a boil and remove from heat
1. Take out a few chestnuts at a time
1. Peel off outer and inner skins while hot
1. Cover shelled chestnuts with water and simmer until tender
1. Drain

#### For Dried Chestnuts

1. Add baking soda to water and bring to a boil
1. Cover dried chestnuts with baking soda water
1. Soak overnight
1. Boil chestnuts in the soaking water until tender
1. Peel skins

## Notes From Gran

* Chestnuts are very good in turkey stuffing
