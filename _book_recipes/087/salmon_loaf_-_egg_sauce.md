# Salmon Loaf - Egg Sauce

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount         | Notes |
| ------------ | -------------- | ----- |
| Salmon       | 1 lb           | Canned - Flaked |
| Lemon Juice  | 1 Tbsp         | |
| Salt         | 1 tsp          | |
| Dry Mustard  | 1 tsp          | |
| Celery       | 1/2 cup        | Chopped |
| Onion        | 1 med          | Chopped |
| Parsley      | 1 Tbsp         | Chopped |
| Eggs         | 2 + 2          | 2 slightly beaten - 2 hard boiled |
| Bread Crumbs | 1 cup          | |
| Milk         | 1 + 1 cup      | |
| Butter       | 2 Tbsp + Extra | |
| Flour        | 2 Tbsp         | |
| Salt         | 1/2 tsp        | |

## Method

#### Make Salmon Loaf
1. Preheat oven to 350F
1. Butter baking dish
1. Flake salmon if not pre-separated
1. Add lemon juice
1. Add salt
1. Add Dry Mustard
1. Chop celery and add
1. Chop onion and add
1. Chop parsley and add
1. Slightly beat 2 eggs and add
1. Add bread crumps
1. Add 1 cup milk
1. Combine after adding each ingredient, in the order given
1. Transfer to buttered baking dish
1. Bake 30 minutes

#### Make Egg Sauce
1. Hard-boil 2 eggs and allow to cool
1. Melt 2 Tbsp butter
1. Mix with 1 cup milk, flour, and salt
1. Cook until thick
1. Slice hard-boiled eggs and stir in

## Notes From Gran

* Serve the salmon loaf with the egg sauce
