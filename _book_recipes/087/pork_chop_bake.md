# Pork Chop Bake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount     | Notes |
| -------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Pork Chops     | 6          | 1 inch thick |
| Potatoes       | 4 med      | Raw, peeled, thinly sliced |
| Mushrooms      | 4 oz       | Sliced, drained |
| Water          | 1 cup      | |
| Onion Soup Mix | 1 envelope | Recipe specifies Lipton brand |
| Butter         |            | For baking dish |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F
1. Peel and thinly slice potatoes
1. Drain and slice mushrooms
1. Brown pork chops in large skillet
1. Drain on paper towels
1. Butter oblong baking dish
1. Arrange potatoes, mushrooms, and chops in baking dish
1. Combine soup mix and water
1. Pour soup mix over meat
1. Cover with foil
1. Bake 1¼ hour or until potatoes are tender
