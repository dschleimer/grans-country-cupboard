# Crab Dewey

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount         | Notes |
| ------------- | -------------- | ----- |
| Lump Crabmeat | 1 lb           | |
| Butter        | 4 + 2 Tbsp     | |
| Green Peppers | 2 Tbsp         | Chopped |
| Mushrooms     | ½ lb (8oz can) | Sliced |
| Pimento       | 1              | Diced |
| Flour         | 2 Tbsp         | |
| Heavy Cream   | 1 cup          | |
| Sherry        | ¼ cup          | |

## Method

1. Sauté crab in 4 Tbsp butter until heated through and set aside
1. Chop Green peppers
1. Slice Mushrooms if not pre-sliced
1. Sauté green pepper and mushrooms in 2 Tbsp butter
1. Add pimento and cream
1. Stir until thickened
1. Add crab and sherry
1. Cook until everything is piping hot but do not let boil.

## Notes From Gran

 * Serve on toast or patty shells
 * Delicious
