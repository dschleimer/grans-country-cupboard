# Molded Tuna Salad

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount  | Notes |
| -------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Lemon Jello          | 3oz pkg | |
| Hot Water            | 1 cup   | |
| Mayonnaise           | 1/2 cup | |
| Pimento Cream Cheese | 5 oz    | |
| Milk                 | 1/2 cup | Recipe calls for "rich milk" presumably whole milk |
| Onion                | 1 Tbsp  | Grated |
| Celery               | 1/2 cup | Finely cut |
| Seasoned Salt        | 1/4 Tsp | |
| Pepper               | 1/4 tsp | Black Pepper |
| Green Pepper         | 2 Tbsp  | Diced |
| Eggs                 | 3       | Hard Boiled - Diced |
| Tuna                 | 14 oz   | Recipe calls for two 7oz size cans of tuna |
| Lettuce              |         | For serving |

## Method

1. Hard Boil eggs and let them cool
1. Grate Onion
1. Finely cut celery
1. Dice Green Pepper and Hard Boiled Eggs
1. Mix Jello and Hot Water
1. Let jell slightly
1. Mix together Mayonnaise, Pimento Cream Cheese, and Milk.  Add to Jello
1. Mix together remaining ingredients and add to Jello mixture
1. Pour into mold
1. Chill several hours
1. Turn mold out onto lettuce

## Notes
* Serves 8-10
