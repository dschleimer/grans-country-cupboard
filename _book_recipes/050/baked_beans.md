# Baked Beans

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount  | Notes |
| ----------- | ------- | ----- |
| Dried Beans | 1½ cups | |
| Dry Mustard | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Salt        | 1 tsp   | |
| Brown Sugar | 3 Tbsp  | |
| Molasses    | 2 Tbsp  | More to Taste |
| Salt Pork   | ¼ lb    | Cut Up |
| Onion       | ¼ cup   | Sliced |

## Method

1. Soak beans overnight in cold water, using twice as much water as beans
1. In morning heat to just below boiling point
1. Cook 2 hours or until shin curls on beans when blown on
1. Preheat oven to 250F
1. Cut up Salt Pork
1. Cut up Onions
1. Drain beans reserving water
1. In a baking crock place alternate layers of beans/seasonings and salt pork
1. Cover with water from beans
1. Bake 6-8 hours, adding water as needed during cooking
