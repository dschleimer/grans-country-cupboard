# Maine Baked Beans

## Ingredients

| Ingredient               | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------------------ | ------- | ----- |
| State of Maine Dry Beans | 2 lbs   | |
| Salt Pork                | ½ lb    | |
| Sugar                    | ¼ cup   | |
| Molasses                 | ⅔ cup   | |
| Dry Mustard              | 2 tsp   | |
| Pepper                   | ½ tsp   | |
| Salt                     | 1½ Tbsp | |
| Onion                    | 1 large | Quartered |
| Maple syrup              | 1 cup   | Optional - Substitute for Molasses if making variant |
| Catsup                   | 1 cup   | Optional - for Variation |
| Vinegar                  | 1 Tbsp  | Optional - for Variation |

## Method

1. Pick over beans
1. Soak overnight in cold water
1. In the morning parboil beans until skins cracks when blown on
1. Preheat oven to 300 °F
1. Quarter Onion and put into bottom of a new pot
1. Add beans to pot on top of onion
1. Put Salt pork on top of beans
1. Boil 1 pint of water in a separate container
1. Mix together Sugar, Dry Mustard, Pepper and Salt with boiling water
1. Add Molasses or Maple Syrup to Sugar Mixture
1. Cut ½ inch through rind of pork
1. Pour sugar mixture on top of Salt Pork
1. If making variation add Catsup and Vinegar to top of pot
1. Cover with boiling water
1. Bake 6 hours or more
1. Add more water as Beans cook

## Notes From Gran
* Serve with steamed brown bread or hot Johnny cakes
