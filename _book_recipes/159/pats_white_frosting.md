# Pat's White Frosting

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Egg Whites       | 3      | |
| Salt             | ¼ tsp  | |
| Light Corn Syrup | ¼ Cup  | |
| Sugar            | 6 Tbsp | |
| Vanilla          | ½ tsp  | |

## Method

1. Beat egg whites with salt until soft peaks form
1. In a small saucepan, combine sugar and corn syrup
1. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the sugar is dissolved and the mixture just reaches a full boil
1. Beat the hot syrup into the egg whites a little at a time
1. Beat in vanilla
1. Continue beating until the mixture holds stiff peaks
1. Spread on the coconut cake
