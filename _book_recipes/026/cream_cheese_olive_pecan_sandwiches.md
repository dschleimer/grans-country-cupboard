# Cream Cheese Olive Pecan Sandwiches

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount  | Notes |
| -------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Cream Cheese   | 8 oz    | |
| Milk           | 3 Tbsp  | |
| Pinento Olives | 1/2 cup | Chopped |
| Pecans         | 1/2 cup | Chopped |
| Pecan Meal     | 1/2 cup | may substitute for Chopped Pecans |

## Method

1. Chop Pecans if using whole rather than meal
1. Chop Pimento Olives
1. Blend all ingredients well
1. Spread

## Notes

* Especially good on toasted bread
