# Cream Cheese Olive Pecan Sandwiches

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount | Notes |
| -------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Cream Cheese   | 8 oz   | |
| Milk           | 3 Tbsp | |
| Pimento Olives | ½ cup  | Chopped |
| Pecans         | ½ cup  | Chopped |
| Pecan Meal     | ½ cup  | may substitute for Chopped Pecans |

## Method

1. Chop Pecans if using whole rather than meal
1. Chop Pimento Olives
1. Blend all ingredients well
1. Spread

## Notes From Gran

* Especially good on toasted bread
