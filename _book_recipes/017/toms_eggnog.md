# Tom's Eggnog

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount | Notes |
| --------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Eggs            | 6      | Separate whites and yolks |
| Sugar           | ¾ cup  | Separate into ½ cup and ¼ cup portions |
| Rich Cream      | 1 pint | |
| Whole Milk      | 1 pint | |
| Blended Whiskey | 1 pint | |
| Jamaican Rum    | 1.5 oz | |
| Nutmeg          |        | Grated - for glasses |

## Method
1. Beat egg yolks
1. Add ½ cup sugar to yolks while beating
1. Stir in cream and milk
1. Add Whiskey and Rum
1. Stir thoroughly
1. Beat egg whites to very stiff peaks
1. Add ¼ cup sugar to egg whites
1. Fold in whites to other ingredients

## Notes From Gran
