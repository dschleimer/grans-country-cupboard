# Tom's Eggnog

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount  | Notes |
| --------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Eggs            | 6       | Separate whites and yolks |
| Sugar           | 3/4 cup | Separate into 1/2 cup and 1/4 cup portions |
| Rich Cream      | 1 pint  | |
| Whole Milk      | 1 pint  | |
| Blended Whiskey | 1 pint  | |
| Jamaican Rum    | 1.5 oz  | |
| Nutmeg          |         | Grated - for glasses |

## Method
1. Beat egg yolks
1. Add 1/2 cup sugar to yolks while beating
1. Stir in cream and milk
1. Add Whiskey and Rum
1. Stir thoroughly
1. Beat egg whites to very stiff peaks
1. Add 1/4 cup sugar to egg whites
1. Fold in whites to other ingredients

## Notes
* Served in antique rose medallion bowl over large piece of ice
