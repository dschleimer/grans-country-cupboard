# Easter Eggnog

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount  | Notes |
| --------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Eggs            | 6       | Separate whites and yolks |
| Sugar           | 3/4 cup | Separate into 1/2 cup and 1/4 cup portions |
| Rich Cream      | 1 pint  | |
| Whole Milk      | 1 pint  | |
| Blended Whiskey | 1 pint  | |
| Jamaican Rum    | 1.5 oz  | |
| Nutmeg          |         | Grated |

## Method
1. Beat egg whites to very stiff peaks
1. Add 1/4 cup sugar tp egg whites
1. Beat egg yolks
1. Add 1/2 cup sugar to yolks while beating
1. Mix the whites with the yolks
1. Stir in cream and milk
1. Add Whiskey and Rum
1. Stir thoroughly

## Notes
1. Serve from punbch bowl
1. Serve with grated nutmeg on each glass
1. Makes 5 pints egg nog
1. Served every Easter after morning church
