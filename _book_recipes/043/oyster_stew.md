# Oyster Stew

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount    | Notes |
| -------------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Oysters              | 1 pint    | shucked |
| Butter               | 1 cup     | |
| Salt                 | ½ tsp     | |
| Cayenne              | Dash      | |
| Pepper               | ⅛ tsp     | |
| Worcestershire Sauce | 1 tsp     | |
| Milk                 | 1½ quarts | "Rich" Milk presumably meaning whole |
| Cream                | 1½ quarts | Optional - may substitute for milk |
| Paprika              | ¼ tsp     | |

## Method

1. Place oysters and juice in saucepan
1. Heat gently until edges of oysters curl
1. Add butter
1. Scald milk or cream and add
1. Heat just to boiling
1. Remove at once from heat
1. Add seasoning
1. Warm soup bowls before serving
1. Place piece of butter in each bowl
1. Pour soup over butter
1. Sprinkle with paprika
