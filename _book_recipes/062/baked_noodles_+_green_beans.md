# Baked Noodles + Green Beans

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Noodles       | 1/2 lb  | Medium Noodles |
| Sour Cream    | 1 cup   | |
| Flour         | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Milk          | 1 cup   | |
| Italian Beans | 9 oz    | Frozen |
| Olives        | 1/3 cup | Ripe Olives - Chopped |
| Salt          | 3/4 tsp | |
| Pepper        | 1/4 tsp | |
| Basil         | 1/2 tsp | |
| Butter        |         | For casserole dish |
| Cheese        | 1 cup   | Swiss or Cheddar - Grated |
| Paprika       | 1/2 tsp | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350F
1. Butter Casserole dish
1. Cook Noodles in salted water
1. Separately cook Beans
1. Drain noodles and beans keeping them separate
1. In a large mixing bowl, blend Sour cream, flour, and milk
1. Stir beans into mixing bowl
1. Chop olives and stir into mixture
1. add salt, pepper and basil, again stirring in
1. Add cooked noodles and mix
1. Turn mixture out into buttered casserole dish
1. Sprinkle with Cheese and Paprika
1. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until heated through
