# Potato Dumplings

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount  | Notes |
| ----------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Egg               | 1       | Beaten |
| Soft Bread Crumbs | ¼ lb    | About 1 slice |
| Flour             | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Chopped Onion     | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Minced Parsley    | ½ tsp   | |
| Salt              | ½ tsp   | |
| Raw Potatoes      | 2½ Cups | Grated |

## Method

1. Combine beaten egg, soft bread crumbs, flour, chopped onion, minced parsley, and salt
1. Stir in 2½ cups grated raw potato
1. With floured fingers, form the mixture into 1½–2 inch balls
1. Dust dumplings lightly with flour before dropping into bubbling stew
1. Cover tight and simmer for 15 minutes

## Modern Notes

* These are hearty German-style potato dumplings (kartoffelklöße)
* Squeeze excess moisture from the grated potatoes before adding to the mixture
* Keep the lid on tight while simmering — lifting it lets the steam escape and the dumplings won't cook through
