# Streusel Fruit Custard

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount | Notes |
| --------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Fruit Cocktail  | 1 can  | 1 lb 13 oz, or sliced peaches or apricot halves, drained |
| Flour           | ¼ Cup  | For custard |
| Sugar           | ¼ Cup  | For custard |
| Sour Cream      | 1 Cup  | For custard |
| Vanilla         | 1 tsp  | For custard |
| Eggs            | 2      | For custard |
| Flour           | ¾ Cup  | For streusel |
| Brown Sugar     | ½ Cup  | For streusel |
| Softened Butter | ¼ Cup  | For streusel |
| Cinnamon        | ½ tsp  | For streusel |

## Method

1. Drain the fruit and arrange in an ungreased 9-inch square pan
1. Mix ¼ cup flour, ¼ cup sugar, sour cream, vanilla, and eggs together
1. Pour the custard mixture over the fruit
1. Mix ¾ cup flour, brown sugar, softened butter, and cinnamon together for the streusel topping
1. Sprinkle the streusel on top before baking
1. Bake at 350°F for 30–40 minutes until golden and set
1. Serve warm with whipped cream
