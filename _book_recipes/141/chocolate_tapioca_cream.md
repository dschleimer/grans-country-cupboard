# Chocolate Tapioca Cream

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount   | Notes |
| --------------------- | -------- | ----- |
| Unsweetened Chocolate | 1 square | |
| Milk                  | 2 Cups   | |
| Instant Tapioca       | 3 Tbsp   | |
| Egg Yolk              | 1        | |
| Sugar                 | ¼ Cup    | |
| Salt                  | ¼ tsp    | |
| Egg White             | 1        | Stiffly beaten |
| Sugar                 | 1 Tbsp   | For egg white |
| Vanilla               | 1 tsp    | |
| Heavy Cream           | ⅓ Cup    | Whipped, for topping |
| Confectioners Sugar   | 2 tsp    | For topping |
| Vanilla               | ½ tsp    | For topping |

## Method

1. Put chocolate, milk, and instant tapioca in a double boiler
1. Cook until the tapioca is clear, stirring occasionally
1. Beat until smooth
1. Mix egg yolk with ¼ cup sugar and ¼ tsp salt
1. Add to the double boiler and cook 2 minutes more
1. Beat egg white stiff with 1 Tbsp sugar
1. Fold the hot tapioca mixture gently into the beaten egg white
1. Add 1 tsp vanilla
1. Chill in serving bowls
1. Top with whipped cream mixed with confectioners sugar and ½ tsp vanilla
