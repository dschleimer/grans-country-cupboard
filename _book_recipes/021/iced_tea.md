# Iced Tea

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Tea        | 6 bags | |
| Water      | 1 gal  | Cold |

## Method

1. Put tea bags into 1 gallon pitcher.
1. Add cold water
1. Let stand 2 hours.  Then ready to drink
1. Keep in refrigerator until finished
1. When finished, throw away tea bags and make a fresh gallon
