# Chicken Cutlets

## Ingredients

| Ingredient               | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------------------ | ------- | ----- |
| Boneless Skinless Chicken Breasts | 6       | |
| Heavy Cream              | 2 cups  | |
| Eggs                     | 4 yolks | |
| Salt                     | 2½ tsp  | |
| White Pepper             | ½ tsp   | |
| Dry Bread Crumbs         | 1 cup   | |
| Butter                   | ¼ lb    | |

## Method

1. Pound Chicken flat
1. Soak in cream for 1 hour
1. Remove from cream
1. Repeat pounding and soaking until chicken has been pounded and soaked 4 times total
1. Drain and shape into cutlets
1. Beat egg yolks together with salt and pepper
1. Dip chicken into egg
1. Roll chicken lightly in bread crumbs
1. Heat butter in skillet
1. Place chicken in butter until lightly browned on all sides and done

## Notes From Gran

* Serve with Asparagus covered with Hollandaise Sauce
* Serves 6
