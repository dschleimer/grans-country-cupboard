# Candied Sweet Potatoes

## Ingredients

| Ingredient     | Amount | Notes |
| -------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sweet Potatoes | 6      | Cooked |
| Butter         | ¼ cup  | |
| Corn Syrup     | ½ cup  | |
| Maple Syrup    | ½ cup  | Optional - May substitute for Corn Syrup |
| Water          | 2 Tbsp | |
| Brown Sugar    | ¼ cup  | |

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 325 °F if baking
1. Cook sweet potatoes
1. Combine ingredients
1. Bake for 1¼ hours, turning at times
1. If using stovetop, cook on top of stove in skillet for 45 minutes
