# Candied Sweet Potatoes

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount  | Notes |
| ------------ | ------- | ----- |
| Sweet Potatoes | 6 | Cooked |
| Butter | 1/4 cup | |
| Corn Syrup | 1/2 cup | |
| Maple Syrup | 1/2 cup | Optional - May subsitute for Corn Syrup |
| Water | 2 Tbsp | |
| Brown Sugar | 1/4 cup | |

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 325F if baking
1. Cook sweet potatoes
1. Combine ingredients
1. Bake for 1 1/4 hours, turning at times
1. If using stovetop, cook on top of stone in skillet for 45 minutes
