# Stuffed Green Peppers

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount   | Notes |
| -------------------- | -------- | ----- |
| Ground Beef          | 1½ lbs   | Recipe calls for Chuck |
| Onions               | 4        | Cut up |
| Green Peppers        | 6        | |
| Worcestershire Sauce | To Taste | |
| Seasoned Salt        | To Taste | |
| Seasoned Pepper      | To Taste | |
| Garlic Powder        | To Taste | |
| Rice                 | 2 cups   | |
| Sugar                | 1 tsp    | |
| Ragu Sauce           | 2 cups   | |
| Cheese               |          | Grated - Optional |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350F
1. Cut tops off green peppers, and cut peppers in half lengthwise
1. Set aside peppers halves
1. Cut up pepper tops
1. Cut up onions
1. Brown beef, onions and cut up pepper tops
1. Cook 2 cups rice in 4 cups water on low heat, with the lid on, for 25 minutes
1. Add rice to beef mixture
1. Add Worcestershire Sauce, Seasoned Salt + Pepper, and Garlic powder to taste
1. Add sugar and Ragu sauce
1. Mix well and fill pepper halves
1. Place stuffed peppers in oven-safe pan
1. Add ½ inch of water
1. Bake for 1 hour
1. If desired, place a little grated cheese on each pepper
