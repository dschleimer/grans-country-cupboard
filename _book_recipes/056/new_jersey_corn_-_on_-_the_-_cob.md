# New Jersey Corn-on-the-Cob

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sweet Corn |        | Freshly Cut |
| Sugar      | 1 tsp  | |
| Butter     |        | For serving |
| Salt       |        | For serving |
| Pepper     |        | For serving |

## Method

1. Hush and silk corn
1. Put in large pot of cold water
1. Add 1 tsp sugar
1. Do *not* salt
1. Bring to a boil - covered
1. Boil 2-3 minutes at most
1. Serve with Butter, Salt, and Pepper

## Notes From Gran

* Boiling corn in salted water will toughen the corn
