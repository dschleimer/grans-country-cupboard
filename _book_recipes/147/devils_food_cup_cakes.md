# Devil's Food Cup Cakes

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount    | Notes |
| ---------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Sugar            | 1 Cup     | |
| Butter           | ½ Cup     | |
| Sifted Flour     | 1 Cup     | |
| Baking Soda      | 1 tsp     | Dissolved in milk |
| Milk             | ½ Cup     | |
| Vanilla          | 1 tsp     | |
| Melted Chocolate | 2 squares | |

## Method

1. Cream sugar and butter together
1. Add sifted flour and mix until the mixture resembles cornmeal
1. Dissolve baking soda in the milk and add along with vanilla
1. Beat until smooth
1. Add melted chocolate and beat well
1. Pour into greased gem pans (cupcake tins)
1. Bake at 325°F for 15–20 minutes

## Notes From Gran

* No icing needed
* Children loved these

## Modern Notes

* "Gem pans" is an old-fashioned term for muffin or cupcake tins -- Claude (AI)
* Two squares of unsweetened chocolate is typically 2 oz -- Claude (AI)
