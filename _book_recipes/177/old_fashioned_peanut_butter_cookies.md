# Old Fashioned Peanut Butter Cookies

## Ingredients

| Ingredient        | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Sugar             | 1 Cup  | Granulated |
| Light Brown Sugar | 1 Cup  | |
| Butter            | 1 Cup  | |
| Peanut Butter     | 1 Cup  | |
| Eggs              | 2      | |
| Vanilla           | 1 tsp  | |
| Flour             | 3 Cups | |
| Baking Powder     | ½ tsp  | |
| Baking Soda       | ¼ tsp  | |

## Method

1. Cream sugars and butter together
1. Add peanut butter and mix well
1. Add eggs and vanilla
1. Sift flour, baking powder, and baking soda together and add
1. Mix well and drop from a spoon on a greased cookie sheet
1. Press with a fork to flatten
1. Bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes
1. Cool on cookie sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to brown paper

## Notes From Gran

* These store well in tight container

## Modern Notes

* The classic fork-press crosshatch pattern is traditional for peanut butter cookies -- Claude (AI)
* Use creamy or chunky peanut butter — either works -- Claude (AI)
* Don't overbake — they firm up as they cool -- Claude (AI)
