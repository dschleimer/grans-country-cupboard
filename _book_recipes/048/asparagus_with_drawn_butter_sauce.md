# Asparagus with Drawn Butter Sauce

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount       | Notes |
| ---------- | ------------ | ----- |
| Asparagus  |              | Fresh |
| Sugar      | Pinch        | |
| Butter     | 2 Tbsp       | |
| Flour      | 1 Tbsp Flour | |
| Milk       | ½ cup        | |
| Salt       | To Taste     | |
| Lemon      |              | For serving |

## Method

1. Wash peel and cut up fresh asparagus
1. Discard tough butts
1. Boil in just enough water to cover
1. When tender, lift asparagus from juice reserving water for later
1. Place asparagus on hot dish
1. In a separate pan melt butter
1. Stir in flour to butter to make a roux
1. Stir in milk to roux
1. Add salt to taste
1. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly
1. Thin with water asparagus was cooked in

## Notes From Gran
* Increase recipe to match amount of asparagus
* Serve with Lemon
