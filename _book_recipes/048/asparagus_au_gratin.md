# Asparagus au Gratin

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Amount     | Notes |
| ------------ | ---------- | ----- |
| Asparagus    | 2 boxes    | |
| Cheese       | 1/2 lb     | Velveeta, American, or Cheddar |
| Butter       | 2/3 cup    | Melted |
| Salt         | To Taste   | |
| Cayenne      | Few grains | |

## Method

1. Cook asparagus as directed
1. Drain asparagus and transfer to greased shallow pan
1. Sprinkle cheese on asparagus
1. Melt butter and pour over cheese
1. Salt to taste
1. Add Cayenne
1. Put under broiler until cheese browns slightly
