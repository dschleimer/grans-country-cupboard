# Asparagus au Gratin

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount     | Notes |
| ------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Asparagus     | 2 boxes    | |
| Velveeta Cheese        | ½ lb       | Optional - use 1/2 lb total of cheese |
| American Cheese        | ½ lb       | Optional - use 1/2 lb total of cheese |
| Cheddar Cheese        | ½ lb       | Optional - use 1/2 lb total of cheese |
| Melted Butter | ⅔ cup      | |
| Salt          | To Taste   | |
| Cayenne       | Few grains | |

## Method

1. Cook asparagus as directed
1. Drain asparagus and transfer to greased shallow pan
1. Sprinkle cheese on asparagus
1. Melt butter and pour over cheese
1. Salt to taste
1. Add Cayenne
1. Put under broiler until cheese browns slightly
