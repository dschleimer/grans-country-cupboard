# Chocolate Butter Icing

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount    | Notes |
| --------------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Butter                | 4 Tbsp    | |
| Unsweetened Chocolate | 2 Squares | |
| Hot Water             |           | |
| Confectioners Sugar   | 2 Cups    | |
| Cocoa                 | 2 Tbsp    | |
| Vanilla               | 1 tsp     | |
| Cream                 |           | As needed for consistency |

## Method

1. Melt 4 Tbsp butter and 2 squares unsweetened chocolate over hot water
1. Stir in 2 cups confectioners sugar and 2 Tbsp cocoa
1. Place over hot water on lowest heat for 10–15 minutes
1. Remove from heat and stir in 1 tsp vanilla
1. If icing thickens, add more cream to keep it smooth and glossy
1. Spread on cake
