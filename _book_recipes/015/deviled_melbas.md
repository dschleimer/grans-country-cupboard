# Deviled Melbas

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Deviled Ham      | 1 can   | Recipe calls for 4.5 oz can, but this seems to be sold in 4.25 oz cans in 2025. |
| Egg              | 1       | Raw - separate yolk from white |
| Prepared Mustard | 1 tsp   | |
| Mayonnaise       | 1/4 cup | |
| Onion            |         | Grated |
| Melba Rounds     |         | |
| Grated Cheese    |         | |

## Method

1. Combine Deviled Ham, egg yolk, mustard, mayonnaise and a little onion
1. Blend together
1. Beat egg white to stiff peaks
1. Fold egg white into ham mixture
1. Spread ham mixture onto Melba rounds
1. Sprinkle with a little grated cheese
1. Broil 3 inches from heat until cheese bubbles
1. Serve at once

## Notes From Gran
* This can be put on party rye too.  (See Modern Notes on [Rye Niblets](/book_recipes/014/rye_niblets.html) for explanation of what party rye.)

## Modern Notes
* Melbas are a kind of twice-baked cracker made from thinly sliced bread.  You can find them as rounds or as "toast" in a rectangular shape.
* Other recipes call for a 4.25 oz can of deviled ham so this may have been a mis-transcribed recipe in the original cookbook. -- David S
