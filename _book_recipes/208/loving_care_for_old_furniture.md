# Loving Care for Old Furniture

## Ingredients

| Ingredient         | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------ | ------ | ----- |
| Boiled Linseed Oil | 1 Part | Equal parts |
| Pure Cider Vinegar | 1 Part | Equal parts |
| Turpentine         | 1 Part | Equal parts |

## Method

1. Mix equal parts boiled linseed oil, pure cider vinegar, and turpentine
2. Apply with soft rag or paint brush to wooden parts only
3. Set it dry outside if possible — it has quite an odor
4. Repeat a couple of times a year
5. The vinegar cleans off all the gunk — linseed oil and turpentine fill up the pores and bring back the original freshness of the wood
