# Eunie's Pecan Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount | Notes |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Dark Karo               | 1 Cup  | |
| Beaten Eggs             | 3      | |
| Vanilla                 | ½ tsp  | |
| Salt                    | ½ tsp  | |
| Melted Butter           | 1 Tbsp | |
| Sugar                   | ½ Cup  | |
| Coarsely Chopped Pecans | 1 Cup  | |
| Unbaked Pie Shell       | 1      | |

## Method

1. Combine Karo syrup, beaten eggs, vanilla, salt, melted butter, and sugar
1. Stir in coarsely chopped pecans
1. Pour into an unbaked pie shell
1. Bake at 450°F for 10 minutes
1. Reduce heat to 350°F and bake 20 minutes more, or until a knife comes out clean when tested in the center

## Notes From Gran

* Try peanuts in place of pecans

## Modern Notes

* Karo is a brand of corn syrup — the dark label variety has a deeper, more molasses-like flavor than the light version -- Claude (AI)
* Starting at a higher temperature sets the crust quickly, then the lower temperature gently cooks the filling through -- Claude (AI)
