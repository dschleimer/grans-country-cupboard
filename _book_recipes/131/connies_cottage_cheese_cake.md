# Connie's Cottage Cheese Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount  | Notes |
| --------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Graham Cracker Crumbs | 1½ Cups | For crust |
| Butter                | ¼ lb    | Melted, for crust |
| Cottage Cheese        | 3 Cups  | Drained and sieved |
| Sugar                 | 1 Cup   | For filling |
| Flour                 | ⅓ Cup   | |
| Salt                  | ½ tsp   | |
| Lemon Juice           | 2 Tbsp  | |
| Vanilla               | 1 tsp   | |
| Eggs                  | 3       | Separated |
| Sugar                 | ½ Cup   | For egg whites |
| Heavy Cream           | 1 Cup   | Whipped |

## Method

1. Mix graham cracker crumbs with melted butter
1. Press evenly on sides and bottom of a lightly greased 9-inch springform pan
1. Combine sieved cottage cheese, 1 cup sugar, flour, and salt
1. Stir in lemon juice and vanilla
1. Beat 3 egg yolks until thick and fold into the cheese mixture
1. Beat 3 egg whites until stiff, gradually beating in ½ cup sugar
1. Fold the beaten whites into the cheese mixture
1. Whip the heavy cream and fold it in
1. Pour into the prepared crust
1. Bake at 300°F until set
1. Turn off the heat and let the cake remain in the oven for 1 hour with the door closed
1. Place on a cake rack in the pan and let cool
1. Remove sides of pan and chill in the refrigerator

## Notes From Gran

* Excellent!

## Modern Notes

* Sieving the cottage cheese gives it a smooth texture similar to ricotta — you can use a food processor instead
* Leaving the cake in the turned-off oven prevents cracking from sudden temperature change
