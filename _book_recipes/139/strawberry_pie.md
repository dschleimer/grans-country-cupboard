# Strawberry Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient              | Amount  | Notes |
| ----------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Fresh Strawberries      | 1 quart | Cut up |
| Sugar                   | 1 Cup   | |
| Lemon Juice             | 1 Tbsp  | |
| Cornstarch              | 3 Tbsp  | |
| Butter                  | ½ Tbsp  | |
| Water                   | 1 Cup   | |
| Baked Pie Shell         | 1       | |
| Sweetened Whipped Cream | 1 Cup   | For topping |

## Method

1. Set aside 1 cup of the cut strawberries
1. Mix 1 cup strawberries with sugar, lemon juice, cornstarch, and butter
1. Add 1 cup water and cook over medium heat, stirring until thick and clear
1. Cool the glaze
1. Stir in the remaining berries, reserving ¼ cup for the top
1. Pour into a baked pie shell
1. Chill until firm, about 3 hours
1. Top with sweetened whipped cream and the reserved ¼ cup berries

## Notes From Gran

* Good with peaches or blueberries

## Modern Notes

* This is a fresh strawberry pie with a cooked glaze — the berries stay mostly fresh and bright
* The cooked portion thickens the filling while the uncooked berries provide fresh flavor and texture
