# Butterscotch Curls

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Flour         | 2 Cups | |
| Baking Powder | 4 tsp  | |
| Salt          | ⅔ tsp  | |
| Shortening    | 4 Tbsp | |
| Milk          | ⅔ Cup  | |
| Butter        | 3 Tbsp | For filling |
| Brown Sugar   | ½ Cup  | For filling |

## Method

1. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt
1. Cut in shortening
1. Add milk and mix with a fork to make a soft dough
1. Knead slightly on a floured board
1. Roll out to ¼ inch thick
1. Spread with 3 Tbsp butter and sprinkle with ½ cup brown sugar
1. Roll up as for a jelly roll
1. Cut into 1-inch slices
1. Stand slices on end in a well-buttered pan or muffin tins
1. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes

## Notes From Gran

* Center of rolls curl up and will be glazed on edges
* Children loved these

## Modern Notes

* These are a quick baking-powder version of cinnamon rolls — no yeast or rising time needed
* Standing the slices on end rather than laying them flat gives the characteristic "curl" as the layers separate during baking
