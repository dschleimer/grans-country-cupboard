# Italian Plum Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Flour         | 1 Cup  | |
| Sugar         | ¾ Cup  | |
| Baking Powder | 1 tsp  | |
| Salt          | ¼ tsp  | |
| Shortening    | ½ Cup  | Cut in |
| Egg           | 1      | Beaten |
| Milk          | ⅓ Cup  | |
| Italian Plums | 2 lbs  | About 9, halved and pitted |
| Sugar         | 4 tsp  | For topping |
| Cinnamon      | 1 tsp  | For topping |
| Butter        |        | For dotting |

## Method

1. Sift flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together
1. Cut in shortening until mixture resembles coarse crumbs
1. Combine beaten egg and milk, then add to dry mix to make a soft dough
1. Spread dough in a greased 8×8-inch pan
1. Wash and cut about 2 lbs Italian plums in half, remove and discard the pits
1. Place plums cut-side up on the dough
1. Mix 4 tsp sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon together and sprinkle over the plums
1. Dot with butter
1. Bake at 375°F for 40–45 minutes

## Notes From Gran

* Try this with other fresh fruits

## Modern Notes

* Italian plums (also called prune plums or Damson plums) are small, oval, and dark purple — they hold their shape well when baked
* This is a classic European-style fruit kuchen with a biscuit-like base
* Fresh peaches, apricots, or nectarines also work well as substitutes
