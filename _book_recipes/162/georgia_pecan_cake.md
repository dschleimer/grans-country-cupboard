# Georgia Pecan Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butter        | 2 Cups | |
| Sugar         | 3 Cups | |
| Eggs          | 6      | Add one at a time, beat after each |
| Flour         | 4 Cups | |
| Baking Powder | 2 tsp  | |
| Salt          | ½ tsp  | |
| Milk          | 1 Cup  | |
| Vanilla       | 2 tsp  | |
| Pecan Meal    | 2 Cups | Finely ground pecans |

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar together
1. Add eggs one at a time, beating after each addition
1. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together
1. Add dry ingredients alternately with milk
1. Stir in vanilla
1. Stir in 2 cups pecan meal
1. Pour into a greased and floured 10-inch tube pan
1. Bake at 325°F for 1½ hours or until done
1. Cool 15 minutes, then remove from pan
1. No icing needed

## Modern Notes

* This is a rich Southern pecan cake — 2 cups of butter and 6 eggs make it very dense and moist -- Claude (AI)
* Pecan meal can be made by pulsing pecans in a food processor until finely ground — be careful not to over-process into butter -- Claude (AI)
