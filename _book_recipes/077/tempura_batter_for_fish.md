# Tempura Batter for Fish

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount     | Notes |
| ------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Flour         | 1 3/4 cups | |
| Egg           | 1          | Well-beaten |
| Water         | 1 3/4 cups | |
| Salt          | dash       | |
| Filet of Fish |            | |

## Method

1. Beat egg well
1. Mix egg, flour, water and salt
1. Refrigerate at least 1/2 hour
1. Trim dark meat from filet
1. Cut filet diagonally into pieces
1. Heat deep oil to 305F - 370F
1. Dip fish in mixture
1. Fry until cooked through
