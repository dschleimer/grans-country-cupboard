# Roast Prime Rib of Beef

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Amount | Notes |
| -------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Rib Roast            | 3 ribs | |
| Worcestershire Sauce |        | |
| Black Pepper         |        | |
| Salt                 |        | |
| Onions               |        | |
| Potatoes             |        | Canned white or quartered brown fresh |
| Onions               |        | |

## Method

1. Have butcher prepare a standing 3 rib roast:
  * cut bones free from meat
  * tie meant and bones, fat side up
1. Preheat oven to 425F
1. Put in roasting pan
1. Shake on a little Worcesterhire Sauce, black pepper, and salt
1. Slice onions in half and pin to fat of meat
1. Insert meat thermometer about center of fat set for beef rare
1. Roast at 425F until meat starts browning
1. Reduce to 400F and roast until done.  3 ribs takes about 2½ hours to cook
1. About 1 hour from done, add potatoes and 1 or 2 cans of onions
1. Stir to brown the sides
1. Do not add water
