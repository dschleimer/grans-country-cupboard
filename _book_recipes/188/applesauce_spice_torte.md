# Applesauce Spice Torte

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                 | Amount | Notes |
| -------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Spice Cake Mix             | 1 pkg  | 2-layer size |
| Fine Graham Cracker Crumbs | 1 Cup  | |
| Frozen Whipped Topping     | 4 Cups | Thawed |
| Applesauce                 | 1 Cup  | |
| Chopped Nuts               | ½ Cup  | |

## Method

1. Prepare cake mix as directed on the package and add graham cracker crumbs to the batter before baking
1. Bake in 2 greased and floured 9-inch layer pans
1. Let cool, then split each layer to form 4 layers
1. Combine 1 cup whipped topping with applesauce and spread between the 3 middle layers
1. Stack the 4 layers, then frost the sides and top with remaining whipped topping
1. Sprinkle with chopped nuts
1. Chill 1 hour before serving
1. Store any remaining cake in the refrigerator

## Modern Notes

* This is a quick icebox-style torte using a cake mix — the graham cracker crumbs add texture
* Use Cool Whip or any frozen whipped topping, thawed
* Must be refrigerated — the whipped topping filling needs to stay cold
