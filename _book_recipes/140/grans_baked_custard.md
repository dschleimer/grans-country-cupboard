# Gran's Baked Custard

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
| ---------- | ------ | ----- |
| Whole Eggs | 4      | |
| Sugar      | ⅓ Cup  | |
| Salt       | ½ tsp  | |
| Vanilla    | 1 tsp  | |
| Rich Milk  | 4 Cups | |
| Nutmeg     |        | A little, for top |

## Method

1. Beat eggs lightly and add sugar, salt, vanilla, and stir slightly
1. Heat milk to scalding and add to the egg mixture, stirring well
1. Pour into individual buttered custard molds, filling nearly full
1. Sprinkle a little nutmeg on top
1. Set cups in a shallow pan and add about 1 inch of hot water to the pan
1. Bake at 350°F for 30–40 minutes, until a knife comes out clean when inserted in the center of the custard
1. Do not overbake
1. Chill and serve

## Modern Notes

* The water bath (bain-marie) ensures gentle, even cooking and prevents the custard from curdling or cracking -- Claude (AI)
* "Rich milk" means whole milk — you can use half-and-half for an even richer custard -- Claude (AI)
