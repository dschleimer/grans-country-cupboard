# Hot Milk Fresh Coconut Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient          | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Eggs                | 4      | |
| Sugar               | 1¾ Cup | |
| Flour               | 2 Cups | |
| Baking Powder       | 2 tsp  | |
| Salt                | ⅛ tsp  | |
| Milk                | 1 Cup  | |
| Butter              | ⅓ Cup  | |
| Lemon Extract       | 1 tsp  | Optional |
| Fresh Coconut       | 1      | For topping |
| Confectioners Sugar |        | For icing |
| Butter              |        | For icing |
| Cream Cheese        |        | For icing |

## Method

1. Melt butter in milk over low heat
1. Beat eggs until light and fluffy, then add sugar
1. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together, then add all at once
1. Add flavoring and the milk-butter mixture, mix well
1. Pour into a greased tube pan
1. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes to 1 hour
1. Grate fresh coconut and pour the coconut milk over the cake when cool
1. Make icing from confectioners sugar, butter, cream cheese, and fresh grated coconut
1. Frost cake and press fresh coconut onto the outside

## Modern Notes

* This is a hot-milk sponge cake topped with fresh coconut — the coconut milk soaks into the cake for extra flavor
* To open a fresh coconut, poke through the eyes to drain the milk, then crack the shell
* Icing amounts are not specified — use about 4 oz cream cheese, ¼ cup butter, and 2 cups confectioners sugar as a starting point
