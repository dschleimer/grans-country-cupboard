# Good Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Amount | Notes |
| ---------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butter           | ½ lb   | |
| Sugar            | 1 Cup  | |
| Eggs             | 3      | Add one at a time |
| Flour            | 3 Cups | |
| Baking Powder    | ½ tsp  | |
| Salt             | ½ tsp  | |
| Vanilla          | 1 tsp  | |
| Sour Cream       | ½ Pint | |
| Baking Soda      | ¼ tsp  | Mixed with sour cream |
| White Raisins    | ½ Cup  | For filling |
| Chopped Nuts     | ½ Cup  | For filling |
| Dark Brown Sugar | ¼ Cup  | For filling |
| Cinnamon         |        | For filling |
| Nutmeg           |        | For filling |
| Orange Juice     | ½ Cup  | For glaze |
| Rum              | ¼ Cup  | For glaze |

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar together
1. Add eggs one at a time, beating after each addition
1. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together
1. Mix sour cream and baking soda together
1. Add dry ingredients and sour cream mixture alternately to the butter mixture
1. Mix white raisins, chopped nuts, dark brown sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg together for the filling
1. Grease the bottom only of an angel cake pan
1. Pour in half of the batter
1. Sprinkle the raisin-nut filling over the batter
1. Pour in the remaining batter and swirl gently
1. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes or until done
1. Cool in the pan, then pour a glaze of orange juice and rum over the top

## Modern Notes

* This is a sour cream coffee cake with a cinnamon-raisin-nut swirl — similar to a Bundt cake
* The orange juice and rum glaze soaks in while the cake is still warm for a moist, flavorful finish
* Use golden raisins (sultanas) for the white raisins
