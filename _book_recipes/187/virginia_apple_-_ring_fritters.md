# Virginia Apple-Ring Fritters

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Apples        | 4–6    | |
| Flour         | 1 Cup  | |
| Baking Powder | 1 tsp  | |
| Salt          | ½ tsp  | |
| Sugar         | 5 Tbsp | |
| Allspice      | ¼ tsp  | |
| Egg           | 1      | Beat slightly |
| Milk          | ½ Cup  | |
| Melted Butter | 1 Tbsp | |
| Lemon Juice   | 1 tsp  | |
| Vegetable Oil |        | For frying |

## Method

1. Sift flour, baking powder, salt, sugar, and allspice together
1. Beat egg slightly, stir in milk and melted butter
1. Add wet ingredients to dry mixture and stir until smooth, then add lemon juice
1. Peel and core apples, then slice into ¼-inch thick rings
1. Dip apple slices in the batter
1. Fry in hot vegetable oil (about ¼ inch deep) until golden brown, turning once
1. Serve with roast pork

## Notes From Gran

* Very good with roast pork

## Modern Notes

* Apple-ring fritters are a classic Virginia side dish, traditionally served with roast pork
* The rings should be about ¼ inch thick — not too thin or they'll fall apart
* Drain well on paper towels and serve immediately while crisp
