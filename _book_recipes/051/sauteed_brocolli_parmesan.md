# Sautéed Brocolli Parmesan

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount   | Notes |
| ---------- | -------- | ----- |
| Brocolli   | 1 head   | May substitute 1 package frozen |
| Salt       | 2 tsp    | |
| Bacon      | 3 strips | Fried in pieces |
| Butter     | 1 Tbsp   | |
| Parmesan   | 4 Tbsp   | |

## Method

1. Soak fresh brocolli in cold salted water for 1 minutes
1. Drain
1. Cook about 20 minutes until almost tender
1. While brocolli is cooking break up bacon into pieces and fry
1. When brocooli is done drain it well
1. Remove bacon from it's grease
1. Add butter to bacon pan
1. Saute brocolli in bacon grease and butter until slightly browned
1. Add bacon pieces and sprinkle with parmesan
