# Almond Cake Roll

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                      | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Flour                           | ¾ Cup  | |
| Baking Powder                   | 1 tsp  | |
| Salt                            | ¼ tsp  | |
| Eggs                            | 4      | Separated |
| Sugar                           | ¾ Cup  | |
| Almond Extract                  | 1½ tsp | |
| Water                           | 2 Tbsp | |
| Finely Chopped Blanched Almonds | ½ Cup  | |
| Whipping Cream                  | 2 Cups | For filling |
| Confectioners Sugar             | ¼ Cup  | For filling |
| Vanilla                         | 1 tsp  | For filling |
| Cherries                        |        | For garnish |
| Citron                          |        | For garnish |
| Almonds                         |        | For garnish |

## Method

1. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together
1. Beat egg yolks until very thick — at least twice
1. Add sugar to yolks, beating until lemon-colored, then add almond extract and beat
1. Add sifted dry ingredients and water
1. Beat egg whites stiff but not dry and gently fold in, adding the chopped almonds slowly
1. Turn into a 10×15×¾-inch pan lined with greased wax paper
1. Bake at 375°F for 15–20 minutes
1. Turn out on a towel dusted with confectioners sugar
1. Strip off wax paper and roll up jelly-roll style quickly — wrap in towel and let cool
1. To fill: whip 2 cups whipping cream with ¼ cup confectioners sugar and 1 tsp vanilla until stiff
1. Unroll cake, fill with half of the whipped cream mixture, roll up gently
1. Spread outside with remaining whipped cream
1. Garnish with a few cut-up cherries, citron, and almonds
1. Serves 10
