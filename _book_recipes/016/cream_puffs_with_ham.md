# Cream Puffs with Ham

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount | Notes |
| --------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Miniature Cream Puffs | 30     | |
| Ham                   | 2 cups | Cooked and Ground |
| Sharp Cheese          | ½ cup  | Grated |
| Catsup                | ½ cup  | Use catsup OR chili sauce.  Not both |
| Chili Sauce           | ½ cup  | Use catsup OR chili sauce.  Not both - recipe calls for homemade |
| Green Pepper          | ¼ cup  | Chopped |
| Olives                | ¼ cup  | Ripe - Chopped |
| Garlic Salt           | ⅛ tsp  | |
| Celery Seed           | ½ tsp  | |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F
1. Slice off the tops of the cream puffs
1. Mix well everything but the cream puffs
1. Put 1 Tbsp of the ham mixture into each puff
1. Place on baking sheet
1. Bake 10-15 minutes

## Notes From Gran
* Serve hot preferably
