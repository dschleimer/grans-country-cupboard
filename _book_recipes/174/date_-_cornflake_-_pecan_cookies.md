# Date - Cornflake - Pecan Cookies

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount | Notes |
| ----------- | ------ | ----- |
| Butter      | ½ Cup  | |
| Sugar       | 1 Cup  | |
| Eggs        | 2      | Separated |
| Flour       | 2 Cups | |
| Baking Soda | ½ tsp  | |
| Salt        | ½ tsp  | |
| Dates       | 1 pkg  | Cut up |
| Pecans      | 2 Cups | In pieces |
| Cornflakes  | 2 Cups | |

## Method

1. Cream butter and sugar together
1. Add egg yolks and beat well
1. Add flour, baking soda, and salt, mix well
1. Beat egg whites stiff and fold in
1. Add dates, pecans, and cornflakes
1. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes

## Modern Notes
