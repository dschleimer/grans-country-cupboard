# Fresh Fruit Salad Dressing

## Ingredients

| Ingredient    | Amount | Notes |
| ------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Vinegar       | 4 Tbsp | |
| Sugar         | 4 Tbsp | |
| Butter        | 2 Tbsp | |
| Eggs          | 2      | Well beaten |
| Whipped cream | 1 cup  | |

## Method

1. Put vinegar, sguar, and butter in saucepan
1. Heat
1. Beat eggs well
1. Add eggs to saucepan and cook slowly until thick
1. Chill
1. Add 1 cup whipped cream
