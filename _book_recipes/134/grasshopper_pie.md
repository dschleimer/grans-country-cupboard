# Grasshopper Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient             | Amount  | Notes |
| ---------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| Chocolate Wafer Crumbs | 1½ Cups | For crust |
| Butter                 | ⅓ Cup   | Melted, for crust |
| Egg Whites             | 3       | |
| Salt                   |         | Dash |
| Sugar                  | 2 Tbsp  | |
| Light Corn Syrup       | ⅓ Cup   | |
| Heavy Cream            | ½ pint  | |
| White Crème de Cacao   | 2 Tbsp  | |
| Green Crème de Menthe  | 2 Tbsp  | |

## Method

1. Mix chocolate wafer crumbs with melted butter
1. Press firmly on bottom and sides of an 8-inch pie plate
1. Beat egg whites with a dash of salt until stiff
1. Add sugar slowly, then the light corn syrup, beating until the meringue is stiff
1. Whip the heavy cream and fold in the crème de cacao and crème de menthe
1. Fold the cream mixture into the egg white meringue
1. Turn into the chocolate crust
1. Freeze for 12 hours or overnight

## Modern Notes

* A Grasshopper Pie is a frozen mint-chocolate dessert named after the Grasshopper cocktail (crème de menthe + crème de cacao + cream)
* The green crème de menthe gives it the classic pale green color
* Keep frozen until ready to serve — let it sit at room temperature for 5–10 minutes before slicing
