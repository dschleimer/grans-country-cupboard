# Key Lime Pie

## Ingredients

| Ingredient               | Amount | Notes |
| ------------------------ | ------ | ----- |
| Egg Yolks                | 3      | Beaten |
| Sweetened Condensed Milk | 1 can  | |
| Lime Juice               | ¾ Cup  | Key lime |
| Graham Cracker Crust     | 1      | 9-inch |
| Egg Whites               | 3      | For meringue |
| Sugar                    | 6 Tbsp | For meringue |
| Salt                     |        | Pinch |

## Method

1. Beat egg yolks until light
1. Add sweetened condensed milk and lime juice
1. Beat until smooth
1. Pour into a 9-inch graham cracker crust
1. Beat egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff
1. Gradually add sugar and beat to make a firm meringue
1. Spread meringue over the filling
1. Bake at 350°F until the meringue is set and lightly browned

## Modern Notes

* This is the classic Key lime pie — the acid in the lime juice reacts with the condensed milk to thicken the filling without baking -- Claude (AI)
* Key limes are smaller and more tart than regular (Persian) limes — bottled Key lime juice works well as a substitute -- Claude (AI)
