# Gourmet Meat-Balls

## Ingredients

| Ingredient             | Amount | Notes |
| ---------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Ground Chuck           | 1½ lbs | |
| Seasoned Salt          | 1 tsp  | |
| Onion                  | 2      | 1 Chopped Fine - 1 Sliced |
| Italian Bread Crumbs   | 1 cup  | |
| Mushrooms              | 8oz    | Recipe calls for one 8oz can of pieces |
| Sherry                 | ¼ cup  | |
| Cream of Mushroom Soup | 1 can  | |
| Sour Cream             | 1 cup  | |
| Butter                 |        | For pan |

## Method

1. Chop one onion fine and slice one onion, keeping chopped and sliced separate
1. Mix together beef, chopped onion, Seasoned salt, and Italian seasoning
1. Make into small meat balls and brown in butter
1. Remove meat balls from skillet and set aside
1. Saute sliced onion and mushroom pieces in the skillet used for meatballs
1. Add sherry and cream of mushroom soup
1. Add the meatballs back and simmer for a few minutes
1. Stir in sour cream just before serving
