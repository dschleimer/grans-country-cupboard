# Eggs à la Maryland

## Ingredients

| Ingredient  | Amount  | Notes |
| ----------- | ------- | ----- |
| Butter      | 1/4 cup | |
| Light Cream | 1 cup   | |
| Crab Meat   | 1 cup   | Flaked |
| Salt        | 1/2 tsp | |
| Pepper      | 1/4 tsp | |
| Red Pepper  | Dash    | |
| Eggs        | 12      | Lightly Beaten |
| Parsley     | 2 Tbsp  | Chopped |

## Method

1. Chop Parsley
1. Lightly beat eggs
1. Melt butter in a large skillet
1. Add Crab, Cream, Salt, Pepper, and Red Pepper
1. Heat through
1. Pour eggs into crab mixture
1. Cook quickly, lifting (with spatula) from bottom and sides as it thickens
1. Cook about 6-8 minutes until eggs are thickened but still moist
1. Sprinkle with Parsley

## Notes

* Serves 6
