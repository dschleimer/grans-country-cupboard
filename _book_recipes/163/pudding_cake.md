# Pudding Cake

## Ingredients

| Ingredient            | Amount | Notes |
| --------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Instant Pudding Mix   | 1      | Lemon flavor |
| Duncan Hines Cake Mix | 1      | Lemon flavor |
| Eggs                  | 4      | |
| Sour Cream            | 1 Cup  | Or milk |
| Cooking Oil           | ¾ Cup  | |
| Powdered Sugar        |        | For dusting |

## Method

1. Beat eggs, then add sour cream
1. Add pudding mix and beat well
1. Add cake mix slowly and beat
1. Beat on high speed for 5 minutes
1. Add cooking oil last and mix in
1. Pour into a greased and floured 10-inch tube pan
1. Bake at 350°F for 45–55 minutes
1. Dust with powdered sugar after baking

## Modern Notes

* This is a classic 1970s "dump cake" style recipe using boxed mixes — the pudding mix makes it extra moist -- Claude (AI)
* Use matching flavors for the pudding and cake mix (lemon with lemon), or mix and match (lemon pudding with yellow cake) -- Claude (AI)
* The sour cream adds richness and tang — don't substitute milk if you can help it -- Claude (AI)
